1 AN ACT concerning

2 State Board of Education – Membership – Employee of a County Board of Education

3 authorizing the Governor to appoint as a member of the State Board of Education
4 an individual who is an employee of a county board of education under certain
5 circumstances; altering the number of members required to
6 affirmatively vote for the State Board to take an action on certain matters; and
7 generally relating to the membership of the State Board of Education.

8 BY repealing and reenacting, with amendments,

10 Section 2–202 and 2–204

16 2–202.

18 appointed by the Governor with the advice and consent of the Senate.
