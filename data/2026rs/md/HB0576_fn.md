State Archives - Record Services - Fees 
 
 
This bill repeals the authorization for the State Archivist to independently establish 
reasonable fees for the care and preservation of records and other services provided by the 
Maryland State Archives (MSA). Instead, the bill establishes that the State Archivist may 
establish fees by regulation. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  None. The change is procedural in nature and does not directly affect 
governmental finances. 
  
Local Effect:  The bill is not anticipated to materially affect local government operations 
or finances. 
 
Small Business Effect:  Minimal. 
 
 
Analysis 
 
Current Law:  Generally, the State Archivist may establish reasonable fees for the care 
and preservation of records and other services provided by MSA. The fees may be in the 
form of (1) a percentage not to exceed 2% of an existing fee charged at the time of the 
creation of a record in any form or format or (2) a flat rate subscription charge for an MSA 
publication. In general, fees are paid to the State Archives Fund, which may be used (1) to 
prepare, edit, and publish volumes of the Archives of Maryland series and (2) for the 
general dissemination of materials. Of the fees collected, 7% must be deposited into the 
Archives Endowment Account of the State Archives Fund as an educational and 
entrepreneurial reserve, which may be used for (1) research on Maryland citizens who

Among other things, MSA may charge (1) a reasonable fee to the State Roads Commission,

posting and maintaining the images of plats; (2) a reasonable fee, not to exceed $3, to the
