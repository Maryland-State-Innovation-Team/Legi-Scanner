Calvert County - County Officials and Board of Education - Salaries and 
Benefits 
 
 
This bill increases, in Calvert County, the salary/compensation of:  (1) the president of the 
board of commissioners, from $50,500 to $58,000; (2) a member of the board of 
commissioners, from $48,000 to $53,000; (3) the president of the board of education, from 
$6,500 to $8,500; (4) a member of the board of education, from $5,500 to $7,500; and  
(5) the county treasurer, from $57,500 to $115,000. Further, the bill authorizes the 
treasurer, beginning with the term of office that begins on or after November 4, 2026, to 
receive the same pension benefits as county employees. Finally, the bill authorizes the 
salary for the Calvert County State’s Attorney to increase during the term of office to the 
extent the salary for a circuit court judge increases by repealing the provision specifying 
that any salary increase takes effect at the beginning of the elected term of office and may 
not increase during the term. Pursuant to constitutional requirements, the increases take 
effect at the beginning of the next terms of office. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  None. 
 
Local Effect:  Calvert County expenditures increase by approximately $52,300 in FY 2027 
and by a minimum of $100,900 annually thereafter, as discussed below. Calvert County 
revenues are not affected. 
 
Small Business Effect:  None.

Analysis 
 
Current Law:  Article III, § 35 of the Maryland Constitution prohibits the salary or 
compensation of any public officer from being increased or diminished during the 
individual’s term of office, except those whose full term is fixed by law in excess of  
four years. According to a letter from the Counsel to the General Assembly, dated  
January 17, 1978, salary increases that are to be implemented over a period of time are 
permissible if implemented before the beginning of the term. 
 
Calvert County Board of Commissioners 
 
Five county commissioners make up the Calvert County Board of Commissioners. The 
annual salary for the president of the Calvert County Board of Commissioners is $50,500. 
The salary for each associate member of the board is $48,000.  
 
Calvert County Board of Education  
 
The Calvert County Board of Education consists of five voting members and one nonvoting 
student member. The board elects from its membership a president and vice president. The 
president of the Calvert County Board of Education receives annual compensation of 
$6,500. The other (voting) members of the board receive annual compensation of $5,500. 
 
State’s Attorney for Calvert County 
 
The State’s Attorney for Calvert County receives an annual salary equal to the salary of a 
circuit court judge; however, any salary increase for the state’s attorney takes effect at the 
beginning of the elected term of office and may not increase during the term. Pursuant to 
Joint Resolutions 2 and 3 of 2022, the salary of a circuit court judge increased from 
$184,433 to the current salary of $214,433 over fiscal 2023 through 2026.  
Senate Joint Resolution 2 and House Joint Resolution 6 of 2026 establish salaries for 
circuit court judges for fiscal 2027 through 2030. Because the General Assembly did not 
act on the resolutions during the requisite time periods, the salaries proposed in the 
resolutions are to be implemented; accordingly, the salary of a circuit court judge is 
$220,333 in fiscal 2027 and increases by $5,900 annually thereafter through fiscal 2030. 
 
Treasurer of Calvert County 
 
The annual salary of the Calvert County Treasurer is $57,500.
