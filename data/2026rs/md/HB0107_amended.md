CHAPTER ______

1 AN ACT concerning

2 Motor Vehicles – Intelligent Speed Assistance System Pilot Program –
3 Establishment

4 requiring certain individuals whose driver’s licenses are subject to
5 certain suspension or revocation to participate in the Program; requiring the Motor
6 Vehicle Administration to issue to participants a restrictive license requiring the use
7 of an intelligent speed assistance system; prohibiting a participant from operating a
8 motor vehicle in violation of the requirements of the Program; requiring the District
9 Court to ensure that certain entries are included in a certain schedule of fines; and
10 generally relating to the Intelligent Speed Assistance System Pilot Program.
