Appropriations 
 
 
Higher Education - Academic Forgiveness Policy - Established 
 
   
This bill requires each institution of higher education that receives State funds to develop 
and implement an academic forgiveness policy by August 1, 2026. The academic 
forgiveness policy must disregard an applicant’s unsatisfactory or failing grades earned at 
a prior institution seven or more years before the applicant submits an application, if the 
applicant chooses to participate in the policy. The bill takes effect July 1, 2026. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  Public institutions of higher education can implement an academic 
forgiveness policy using existing resources. Revenues are not affected. 
  
Local Effect:  Local community colleges can implement an academic forgiveness policy 
using existing resources. Revenues are not affected.  
  
Small Business Effect:  None.

Transfers between institutions of higher education in Maryland are covered by the 
Maryland Student Transfer Policy, which governs the transfer of credits and other

Additional Comments:  Nonprofit higher education institutions that receive State funds 
can likewise implement an academic forgiveness policy with existing resources.
