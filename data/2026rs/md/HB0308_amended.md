2 HOUSE BILL 308

1 Article – Corporations and Associations
2 Section 1–101(w)
3 Annotated Code of Maryland

5 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND,
6 That the Laws of Maryland read as follows:

7 Article – Corporations and Associations

8 1–101.

9 (H–1) “COMMERCIAL MAIL RECEIVING AGENCY” MEANS A PERSON THAT IS
10 AUTHORIZED BY THE U.S. POSTAL SERVICE TO ACCEPT THE DELIVERY OF MAIL ON
11 BEHALF OF ANOTHER PERSON AS A BUSINESS SERVICE.

12 (w) “Principal office” means:

13 (1) The place in this State filed or recorded with the Department as the
14 principal office of a Maryland corporation, limited liability company, limited liability
15 partnership, or limited partnership; [or]

16 (2) AN ADDRESS IN THIS STATE SUPPLIED BY A COMMERCIAL MAIL
17 RECEIVING AGENCY WITH WHICH A MARYLAND CORPORATION, LIMITED LIABILITY
18 COMPANY, LIMITED LIABILITY PARTNERSHIP, OR LIMITED PARTNERSHIP HAS A
19 VALID COMMERCIAL MAIL RECEIVING AGREEMENT IN ACCORDANCE WITH U.S.
20 POSTAL SERVICE REGULATIONS; OR

21 (3) If there is no principal office designated UNDER ITEM (1) OF THIS
22 SUBSECTION OR ADDRESS SUPPLIED UNDER ITEM (2) OF THIS SUBSECTION, the main
23 office of the Maryland corporation, limited liability company, limited liability partnership,
24 or limited partnership in this State for the transaction of business.

25 SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That this Act shall take effect
26 October 1, 2026.
