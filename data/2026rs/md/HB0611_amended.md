3 and Advertisement Restrictions

4 PG 317–26

5 FOR the purpose of increasing certain application, license, permit, and license transfer fees
6 related to alcoholic beverages in Prince George’s County; providing that the Board of License
7 Commissioners for Prince George’s County may not issue, transfer, or renew a certain license or
8 permit unless certain ownership and operational requirements are met; requiring the holders of
9 certain licenses or permits issued before the effective date of this Act to comply with the
10 ownership and operational requirements under this Act on or before a certain date;
11 prohibiting certain license holders from displaying on the exterior or windows of the
12 licensed premises certain advertisements, subject to a certain exception; and generally relating to
13 alcoholic beverages in Prince George’s County.

5 26–1609.

6 (A) IN THIS SECTION, “BENEFICIAL OWNER” MEANS AN INDIVIDUAL
7 WHO:

8 (1) HAS A DIRECT OR INDIRECT OWNERSHIP INTEREST IN A
9 LICENSED ESTABLISHMENT;

10 (2) IS ENTITLED TO RECEIVE PROFITS, PROCEEDS, OR
11 DISTRIBUTIONS FROM THE LICENSED ESTABLISHMENT; AND

12 (3) HAS THE POWER TO DIRECT OR CONTROL THE MANAGEMENT,
13 POLICIES, OR OPERATIONS OF THE LICENSED ESTABLISHMENT.

14 (B) THIS SECTION APPLIES TO:

15 (1) A CLASS A BEER LICENSE;

16 (2) A CLASS A BEER AND LIGHT WINE LICENSE;

17 (3) A CLASS A BEER, WINE, AND LIQUOR LICENSE;

18 (4) A CLASS B–PLUS BEER, WINE, AND LIQUOR LICENSE; AND

19 (5) A SUNDAY OFF–SALE PERMIT ISSUED UNDER § 26–1104 OF
20 THIS TITLE IN CONNECTION WITH A LICENSE DESCRIBED IN ITEMS (1) THROUGH
21 (4) OF THIS SUBSECTION.

22 (C) THE BOARD MAY ISSUE, TRANSFER, OR RENEW A LICENSE OR
23 PERMIT SUBJECT TO THIS SECTION ONLY IF:

24 (1) THE APPLICANT OR TRANSFEREE IS A BENEFICIAL OWNER OF
25 THE LICENSED ESTABLISHMENT;

26 (2) EACH BENEFICIAL OWNER OF THE LICENSED ESTABLISHMENT:

27 (I) IS DISCLOSED TO THE BOARD;

28 (II) SATISFIES ALL REQUIREMENTS FOR A LICENSE HOLDER
29 UNDER THIS ARTICLE; AND

30 (III) IS APPROVED BY THE BOARD;

31 (3) THE APPLICANT OR TRANSFEREE OWNS AT LEAST 51% OF THE
32 LICENSED ESTABLISHMENT; AND

33 (4) THE APPLICANT OR TRANSFEREE EXERCISES DAY–TO–DAY
34 OPERATIONAL CONTROL OVER THE LICENSED ESTABLISHMENT.

35 (D) THE BOARD MAY NOT ISSUE, TRANSFER, OR RENEW A LICENSE OR
36 PERMIT SUBJECT TO THIS SECTION IF:

37 (1) THE APPLICATION FOR THE LICENSE IS MADE ON BEHALF OF
38 AN UNDISCLOSED BENEFICIAL OWNER;

39 (2) A PERSON WHO IS NOT DISCLOSED IN THE APPLICATION HAS A
40 DIRECT OR INDIRECT OWNERSHIP INTEREST IN OR EXERCISES DIRECT OR
41 INDIRECT CONTROL OVER THE LICENSED ESTABLISHMENT;

42 (3) THE LICENSED ESTABLISHMENT IS OPERATED UNDER A
43 MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT, FINANCING AGREEMENT, PROFIT–SHARING
44 AGREEMENT, CONSULTING AGREEMENT, OR OTHER AGREEMENT THAT
45 TRANSFERS THE RESPONSIBILITIES OF A BENEFICIAL OWNER TO A PERSON NOT
46 APPROVED BY THE BOARD; OR

47 (4) THE APPLICANT OR ANY BENEFICIAL OWNER FAILS TO MEET
48 THE QUALIFICATIONS REQUIRED OF A LICENSE HOLDER OR PERMIT HOLDER
49 UNDER THIS ARTICLE.

50 (E) THE BOARD MAY REVOKE A LICENSE OR PERMIT SUBJECT TO THIS
51 SECTION FOR NONCOMPLIANCE WITH THE REQUIREMENTS OF THIS SECTION.

52 (F) THE BOARD MAY ADOPT REGULATIONS TO IMPLEMENT THIS
53 SECTION, INCLUDING REGULATIONS THAT REQUIRE THE PRODUCTION OF SWORN
54 DISCLOSURES, AFFIDAVITS, OPERATING AGREEMENTS, SHAREHOLDER
55 AGREEMENTS, PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENTS, FINANCING AGREEMENTS,
56 MANAGEMENT AGREEMENTS, TAX DOCUMENTS, AND ANY OTHER RECORDS
57 NECESSARY TO DETERMINE BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP OF A LICENSED
58 ESTABLISHMENT.

78 (A) (1) IN THIS SECTION, “ADVERTISEMENT” MEANS ANY VISUAL,

80 TO INDUCE THE SALE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES, TOBACCO, OR VAPING
81 PRODUCTS.

82 (2) “ADVERTISEMENT” DOES NOT INCLUDE PACKAGING OR
83 LABELING.

84 (B) (1) EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN PARAGRAPH (2) OF THIS
85 SUBSECTION, THIS SECTION APPLIES TO THE HOLDER OF:

86 (I) A CLASS A BEER LICENSE;

87 (II) A CLASS A BEER AND LIGHT WINE LICENSE;

88 (III) A CLASS A BEER, WINE, AND LIQUOR LICENSE;

89 (IV) A CLASS B–PLUS BEER, WINE, AND LIQUOR LICENSE;
90 AND

91 (V) A CLASS D BEER AND LIGHT WINE LICENSE.

92 (2) THIS SECTION DOES NOT APPLY TO LICENSED PREMISES IN
93 THE 23RD LEGISLATIVE DISTRICT.

94 (C) A LICENSE HOLDER MAY NOT DISPLAY ANY ADVERTISEMENT ON THE
95 EXTERIOR OF THE LICENSED PREMISES OR ON THE WINDOWS OF THE LICENSED
96 PREMISES.

97 (D) A PERSON WHO VIOLATES THIS SECTION IS GUILTY OF A
98 MISDEMEANOR AND ON CONVICTION IS SUBJECT TO A FINE NOT EXCEEDING
99 $1,000.

100 SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That:

102 Prince George’s County shall study the use of memoranda of understanding between the
103 Board, community associations, and applicants for or holders of an alcoholic beverages
104 license and report to the members of the Prince George’s County delegation to the
105 General Assembly, in accordance with § 2–1257 of the State Government Article, on the

107 (b) The scope of the study shall include:

108 (1) the use of memoranda of understanding in counties and
109 municipalities in the State as well as other states;

110 (2) the subject matter of the memoranda of understanding;

111 (3) any laws, regulations, or policies that govern the use and contents
112 of the memoranda of understanding; and

113 (4) any other information that the Board considers relevant.
