Anne Arundel County - Sheriff - Appointment of Majors 
 
 
This bill authorizes the Anne Arundel County Sheriff to appoint three majors who serve at 
the pleasure of the sheriff at a salary to be determined by the Anne Arundel County 
Council. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  The bill does not materially affect State operations or finances. 
 
Local Effect:  Anne Arundel County expenditures may increase by as much as $822,300 in 
FY 2027 and by at least $730,000 annually thereafter, as discussed below. Revenues are 
not affected.  
 
Small Business Effect:  None. 
 
 
Analysis 
 
Current Law:  The Sheriff of Anne Arundel County is required to appoint deputies at a 
salary provided by the Anne Arundel County Council. Additionally, the sheriff is 
authorized to appoint a chief deputy who serves at the discretion of the sheriff. 
 
Local Expenditures:  Because the bill is authorizing in nature, the ultimate impact on 
Anne Arundel County expenditures depends on action taken by the county.
