1 AN ACT concerning

2 Alcoholic Beverages – Class 3 Wineries and Class 4 Limited Wineries – Bulk
3 Wine AND POMACE BRANDY

4 FOR the purpose of expanding the definition of “pomace brandy” authorized to be
5 produced and sold by the holder of a Class 4 limited winery license; authorizing the
6 holder of a Class 3 winery license to acquire bulk wine from the holder of a Class 4
7 limited winery license; authorizing the holder of a Class 4 limited winery license to sell
8 and deliver bulk wine to the holder of a Class 3 winery license; clarifying that the holder
9 of a Class 4 limited winery license must report all pomace brandy distilled and bottled
10 by the holder as a distilled spirit for purposes of the alcoholic beverage tax; and
11 generally relating to Class 3 wineries and Class 4 limited wineries.
