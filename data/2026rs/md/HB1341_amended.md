1 AN ACT concerning

2 Immigration Enforcement – Expanding Sensitive Locations, Notification, and
3 Guidance
4 (The Maryland Values Act of 2026)

5 FOR the purpose of requiring certain public school personnel to notify a county
6 superintendent or a county superintendent’s designee if the individual receives
7 certain notification of certain immigration enforcement at a school; prohibiting
8 public school personnel from engaging in certain immigration enforcement;
9 prohibiting public school personnel from producing or sharing certain student or
10 employee information for purposes of immigration enforcement, except under certain
11 circumstances; expanding the definition of sensitive locations relating to the
12 definition of a “public school”; expanding the definition of sensitive locations to
13 include certain locations that distribute food to individuals in need; requiring certain
14 courthouses to implement a certain policy; requiring the Attorney General to update
15 certain guidance on or before a certain date each year and certain courthouses, public
16 schools, public libraries, and units of the Executive Branch of State and local
17 government to update certain policies; and generally relating to immigration
18 enforcement.
