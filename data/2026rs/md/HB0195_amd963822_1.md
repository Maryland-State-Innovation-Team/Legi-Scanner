AMENDMENTS TO HOUSE BILL 195
(Third Reading File Bill)

AMENDMENT NO. 1
On page 1, in line 20, after “State;” insert “requiring the State of Maryland to
become a Contributing Member of the National Council of Legislators from Gaming
States;”.

AMENDMENT NO. 2
On page 6, after line 8, insert:

“SECTION 3. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That the State of Maryland
shall become a Contributing Member of the National Council of Legislators from
Gaming States with the following representatives:

(1) one member of the Senate of Maryland, appointed by the President
of the Senate; and

(2) one member of the House of Delegates, appointed by the Speaker of
the House.”;

and in line 9, strike “3.” and substitute “4.”.
