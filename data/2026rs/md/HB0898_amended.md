CHAPTER ______

1 AN ACT concerning

2 Economic Development – Delivering Economic Competitiveness and Advancing
3 Development Efforts (DECADE) Act

4 FOR the purpose of altering the designation, administration, and purposes of and eligibility
5 for certain economic development programs; redesignating the Economic
6 Development Opportunities Program Account to be the Strategic Closing Fund
7 within the Department of Commerce; altering the purposes for and methods by
8 which the Strategic Closing Fund may be utilized; altering the distribution of certain video
9 lottery terminal proceeds; providing for certain requirements relating to the
10 administration of the Small, Minority, and Women–Owned Businesses Account;
11 providing that the sales and use tax does not apply to the sale of certain information
12 technology services and certain digital codes and digital products under certain
13 circumstances; altering the termination date of the Build Our Future Grant Pilot Program,
14 Job Creation Tax Credit, Research and Development Tax Credit, and Employer Security
15 Clearance Costs Tax Credit; altering eligibility for and the calculation of certain tax
16 credits; establishing the purpose of the film production activity tax credit; authorizing a
17 qualified film production entity to amend its application for the film production activity tax
18 credit under certain circumstances; altering certain reporting requirements concerning
19 certain economic development programs and tax credits; requiring the Office of the
20 Comptroller and the Department of Commerce to evaluate the film production activity tax
21 credit and submit a certain report on or before a certain date; and generally relating to
22 economic development and economic development initiatives.
