Department of Information Technology - Workgroup on the Medicaid 
Management Information System Mainframe System Replacement 
 
   
This bill requires the Department of Information Technology (DoIT) to convene a 
workgroup on the replacement of the Medicaid Management Information System (MMIS) 
mainframe system. The workgroup must assist the Maryland Department of Health (MDH) 
in procuring and transitioning to a replacement MMIS mainframe system with specified 
functionality by July 1, 2029. By September 1, 2026, and every three months thereafter 
through September 1, 2029, the workgroup must report to specified committees of the 
General Assembly on the progress made toward procuring a replacement MMIS mainframe 
system. The workgroup must also submit a report annually for three years to specified 
committees of the General Assembly, beginning January 1, 2027, on the activities of the 
workgroup, including a timeline for meeting the implementation deadline for replacement. 
The bill takes effect June 1, 2026, and terminates June 30, 2030. 
   
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  DoIT can convene the workgroup using existing budgeted resources. MDH 
and other State entities can participate in the workgroup with existing budgeted resources. 
Any costs associated with replacing the MMIS mainframe system are not affected directly 
by the bill but may be affected by decisions made by the workgroup. Revenues are not 
affected. 
 
Local Effect:  None. 
 
Small Business Effect:  None.

Analysis 
 
Bill Summary:  A replacement mainframe system for MMIS must (1) promote payment 
accuracy, system security and performance, and responsiveness and modernization of the 
system’s outdated technological infrastructure and (2) support, at a minimum, the 
functionalities of the existing system, including provider enrollment; medical, dental, and 
pharmacy claims processing; beneficiary inquiries; data reporting; and audit functions in 
accordance with the federal Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services and other 
applicable federal requirements. 
 
The workgroup must consist of at least one representative from MDH, the Department of 
Human Services, the Maryland Health Benefit Exchange, and the Office of the 
Comptroller, as well as two individuals with experience in health insurance policy. The 
workgroup may consult with experts as necessary in conducting its duties. 
 
Current Law:  MMIS is an integrated group of procedures and computer processing 
operations (subsystems). Under federal regulations, the objectives of MMIS include 
program control and administrative costs; service to recipients, providers, and inquiries; 
operations of claims control and computer capabilities; and management reporting for 
planning and control. States may receive 90% federal financial participation (FFP) for 
design, development, or installation, and 75% FFP for operation of state mechanized claims 
processing and information retrieval systems. 
 
Planning to replace the legacy MMIS system (which dates to the 1990s) first began in 2008, 
but the process never advanced to the development or procurement stage and remained 
dormant for many years. At the time, the estimated cost to replace the legacy system was 
$111 million. MDH advises that it has now established an overall MMIS modernization 
roadmap, including MMIS replacement. MDH has neither requested nor received State or 
federal funding for a replacement MMIS. Funding associated with project planning for 
MMIS replacement has been approved for fiscal 2027.  
 
Additional Comments:  Under the bill, the workgroup must report quarterly (from 
September 1, 2026, through September 1, 2029) on the progress made toward procuring an 
MMIS mainframe system replacement. DoIT advises that reporting once every six months 
should be more than sufficient to advise on the workgroup’s progress.
