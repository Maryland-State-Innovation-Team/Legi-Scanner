Vehicle Laws - Electric Vehicle Dealer Licenses - City of Bowie 
 
  
This bill increases, from four to five, the number of dealer licenses available for certain 
manufacturers or distributors that deal only in electric or nonfossil-fuel burning vehicles. 
The bill further specifies that one of these five licenses must be issued for the operation of 
a dealership in the City of Bowie.  
   
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  The bill is not expected to materially affect State finances or operations.  
  
Local Effect:  Assuming the establishment of a new electric vehicle dealership,  
City of Bowie property tax revenues increase by approximately $12,000 annually, as 
discussed below. Expenditures are not affected.  
  
Small Business Effect:  Minimal. 
  
 
Analysis 
 
Current Law:  Generally, a manufacturer or distributor may not sell a new vehicle to a 
retail buyer. However, a vehicle manufacturer or distributor may be licensed as a dealer if 
(1) the licensee deals only in electric or nonfossil-fuel burning vehicles; (2) no other dealer 
holds a franchise from the manufacturer or distributor; and (3) there is no cross-ownership 
between licensees, whereby a licensee – or any entity affiliated with or controlled by a 
licensee – holds a controlling interest in another licensee or other entity affiliated with or 
controlled by a licensee. The Motor Vehicle Administration may only issue four of these 
licenses. Statute does not specify the locations for dealerships operating under these 
licenses.

Local Revenues:  According to the City of Bowie, assuming a $3,000,000 assessed value 
for a new electric vehicle dealer and applying the city’s property tax rate of $0.40 per 
$100 of assessed value, the bill may generate up to $12,000 of tax revenue each year.   
 
 
Additional Information 
 
Recent Prior Introductions:  Similar legislation has not been introduced within the last 
three years.
