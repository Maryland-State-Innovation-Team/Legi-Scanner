AMENDMENTS TO HOUSE BILL 1164
(Third Reading File Bill)

AMENDMENT NO. 1
On page 1, strike beginning with “applying” in line 9 down through the second
“companies” in line 11 and substitute “authorizing the Public Service Commission to
require a water company, sewage disposal company, or water and sewage disposal
company to adopt a certain limited–income mechanism”; and in lines 11 and 12, strike
“Public Service”.

AMENDMENT NO. 2
On page 4, in line 19, strike the colon; in lines 20, 21, and 22, strike “1.”, “2.”, and
“3.”, respectively; in lines 20 and 21, in each instance, strike the brackets; in the same
lines, in each instance, strike the semicolon; and strike beginning with the semicolon in
line 22 down through “COMPANY” in line 25.

On page 5, after line 11, insert:

“(4) THE COMMISSION MAY REQUIRE A WATER COMPANY, SEWAGE
DISPOSAL COMPANY, OR WATER AND SEWAGE DISPOSAL COMPANY TO ADOPT A
LIMITED–INCOME MECHANISM SUBJECT TO THE APPROVAL OF THE COMMISSION
IN THE SAME MANNER AS A UTILITY COMPANY IN ACCORDANCE WITH THIS
SECTION.”.
