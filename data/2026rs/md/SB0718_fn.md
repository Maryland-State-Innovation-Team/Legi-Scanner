Frederick County - Alcoholic Beverages - Class L Permit 
 
 
This bill establishes a Class L beer, wine, and liquor permit in Frederick County and 
authorizes the Frederick County Board of License Commissioners to issue the permit to 
the holder of a manufacturer’s license that also holds a class DBR brewery license or a 
class DDS distillery permit. The permit authorizes the permit holder to sell by the glass for 
on‑premises consumption, beer, wine, liquor, and mixed drinks from products 
manufactured by the permit holder or another holder of a manufacturer’s license. The 
permit also allows for the tasting of beer, wine, or liquor, or a combination of the three, at 
no cost and in quantities, as specified. The hours of sale or tasting under the permit are the 
same as those for the required manufacturer’s license, and the permit holder must comply 
with alcohol training requirements under State law. The board must adopt regulations to 
carry out the bill. The annual permit fee is $250. The bill takes effect July 1, 2026. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  This bill is not expected to materially affect State finances or operations. 
  
Local Effect:  Frederick County revenues increase, beginning in FY 2027, to the extent 
that Class L permits are issued under the bill. Frederick County can handle any additional 
enforcement activity with existing resources. 
  
Small Business Effect:  Minimal. 
  
 
Analysis
