Howard County - Sheriff and State's Attorney - Compensation  
Ho. Co. 13-26 
 
   
This bill alters the salaries of the Howard County Sheriff and the Howard County State’s 
Attorney by establishing the following salaries for each office:  (1) for calendar 2027, 
$220,601; (2) for calendar 2028, $227,230; (3) for calendar 2029, $234,079; and (4) for 
calendar 2030 and annually thereafter, $241,149. Pursuant to constitutional requirements, 
the salary increases take effect at the beginning of the next terms of office. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  The bill does not materially affect State operations or finances. 
  
Local Effect:  Howard County expenditures increase by approximately $48,000 in  
FY 2027 and by at least $96,000 annually thereafter, as discussed below. County revenues 
are not affected. 
  
Small Business Effect:  None. 
  
 
Analysis 
 
Current Law:  Under statute, the sheriff’s salary is equal to the salary of a major on  
step 6 of the Howard County Police Management Schedule and the state’s attorney’s salary 
is equal to the salary of a District Court Judge. Accordingly, the state’s attorney’s salary in 
fiscal 2026 is $201,333; according to Howard County, the current salary of the sheriff is 
$138,671 (the Department of Legislative Services notes that the reported salary appears to 
differ from that under the management schedule).

Article III, § 35 of the Maryland Constitution prohibits the salary or compensation of any 
public officer from being increased or diminished during the individual’s term of office, 
except those whose full term is fixed by law in excess of four years. According to a letter 
from the Counsel to the General Assembly, dated January 17, 1978, salary increases that 
are to be implemented over a period of time are permissible if implemented before the 
beginning of the term. 
 
Local Expenditures:  Howard County expenditures increase by approximately $48,000 in 
fiscal 2027, by $96,00 in fiscal 2028, and by at least $104,000 annually thereafter. Pursuant 
to constitutional requirements, the salaries are not altered until the next terms of office. 
 
This estimate (1) reflects future salary amounts for the state’s attorney that would have 
otherwise occurred beginning in fiscal 2027 (i.e., salary amounts for District Court judges 
as implemented in Senate Joint Resolution 2/House Joint Resolution 6 of 2026); (2) reflects 
only the current salary of the sheriff as reported by Howard County; and (3) does not 
account for fringe benefits. To the extent that the sheriff would have – in the absence of 
the bill – received salary increases based on the current salary structure in the county, the 
expenditures attributable to the bill and reflected in this analysis are mitigated. 
 
 
Additional Information 
 
Recent Prior Introductions:  Similar legislation has not been introduced within the last 
three years. 
 
Designated Cross File:  None.
