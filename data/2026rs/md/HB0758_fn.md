Ways and Means 
 
 
Problem Gambling - Authorized Uses of Funds - Alterations 
 
 
This bill expands the authorized uses of the Problem Gambling Fund to include providing 
reimbursement to support peers providing behavioral health services to individuals with 
problem gambling issues who are receiving treatment or participating in prevention 
programs. The bill also restricts authorized uses of State and federal funds – including 
funds from the Problem Gambling Fund – such that they may not be used for research and 
training designed to improve or extend specified services for problem gamblers. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  The bill primarily alters the purposes for which existing funds may be used. 
To the extent it requires administrative changes, the Maryland Department of Health 
(MDH) can handle them with existing resources. 
 
Local Effect:  None.   
 
Small Business Effect:  None. 
 
 
Analysis 
 
Current Law:   
 
Problem Gambling Fund 
 
The Problem Gambling Fund is a special, nonlapsing fund in MDH intended to provide 
funding for problem gambling treatment and prevention programs, including inpatient and 
residential services, outpatient services, intensive outpatient services, continuing care

Expenditures from the fund must be made only by MDH to (1) establish a 24‑hour hotline 
for counseling and other support services for compulsive and problem gamblers; 
(2) establish an outreach program for compulsive and problem gamblers; (3) develop and 
implement free or reduced‑cost problem gambling treatment and prevention programs; and 
(4) develop and implement free or reduced‑cost problem gambling treatment and 
prevention programs targeted to individuals with problem gambling issues related to sports 
wagering, participation in fantasy competitions, and other forms of wagering conducted 
through online means. After funding these items, any unspent funds may be expended by 
MDH on drug and other addiction treatment services. 
 
The special fund is capitalized with annual fees paid by video lottery operation licensees 
on each of their video lottery terminals and table games in use during the year. The 
fiscal 2027 budget as introduced includes $6.1 million in special fund expenditures from 
the fund. 
 
Network of Clinically Appropriate Services to Problem Gamblers 
 
The Secretary of Health must establish a network of clinically appropriate services to 
problem gamblers throughout the State. The Secretary must make grants from or 
agreements for the use of State funds, including the funds provided through the Problem 
Gambling Fund, and federal funds to help public agencies or nonprofit organizations 
operate the network of clinically appropriate services for problem gamblers who reside in 
Maryland to provide inpatient and residential services, outpatient services, intensive 
outpatient services, continuing care services, educational services, services for victims of 
domestic violence, and other preventive or rehabilitative services or treatment. Research 
and training that are designed to improve or extend these services are proper items of 
expense. 
 
 
Additional Information 
 
Recent Prior Introductions:  Similar legislation has not been introduced within the last 
three years.
