AMENDMENT TO HOUSE BILL 829
(Third Reading File Bill)

On page 4, in lines 25 and 26, strike “CONNECT AN INDIVIDUAL WHO MAY BE
OR IS A VICTIM OF HUMAN TRAFFICKING WITH AVAILABLE RESOURCES” and
substitute “REPORT SUSPECTED INCIDENTS OF HUMAN TRAFFICKING TO
APPROPRIATE AUTHORITIES”.

On page 6, in line 23, strike “JULY” and substitute “JUNE”; and in the same line,
strike “SEPTEMBER” and substitute “NOVEMBER”.

On page 7, in line 2, strike “TRAFFICKING/COMBATTING” and substitute
“TRAFFICKING/COMBATING”; in line 3, after “TRANSPORTATION” insert “SECTOR”;
in line 15, strike “CONNECT AN INDIVIDUAL WHO MAY BE OR IS A VICTIM” and
substitute “REPORT SUSPECTED INCIDENTS”; and in line 16, strike “WITH
AVAILABLE RESOURCES” and substitute “TO APPROPRIATE AUTHORITIES”.
