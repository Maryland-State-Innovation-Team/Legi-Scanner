1 AN ACT concerning

2 Higher Education – Student Financial Assistance – Dependents of State or
3 Local Public Safety Employees
4 (Maryland Fallen Heroes Tuition Benefits Act)

5 FOR the purpose of exempting spouses and financially dependent children of certain State or local
6 public safety employees from paying out–of–state or out–of–county tuition at a public
7 institution of higher education in the State; altering the method for allocating funds for the
8 Edward T. and Mary A. Conroy Memorial Scholarship Program and the Jean B. Cryor
9 Memorial Scholarship Program; and generally relating to student financial assistance for
10 spouses and financially dependent children of State or local public safety employees.

3 (A) IN THIS SECTION, “STATE OR LOCAL PUBLIC SAFETY EMPLOYEE” HAS
4 THE MEANING STATED IN § 18–601 OF THIS ARTICLE.

5 (B) NOTWITHSTANDING THE STATE OR COUNTY IN WHICH THE SPOUSE OR THE CHILD
6 RESIDES, A SPOUSE OR A FINANCIALLY DEPENDENT CHILD OF A STATE OR LOCAL PUBLIC SAFETY
7 EMPLOYEE WHO DIES AS A RESULT OF OR IN THE COURSE OF PERFORMANCE OF
8 DUTIES IS EXEMPT FROM PAYING OUT–OF–STATE OR OUT–OF–COUNTY TUITION AT
9 A PUBLIC INSTITUTION OF HIGHER EDUCATION IN THE STATE.

10 (C) THE COMMISSION SHALL ADOPT REGULATIONS TO IMPLEMENT THIS
11 SECTION.

12 18–601.

13 (a) (1) In this section the following words have the meanings indicated.

14 (2) “Disabled public safety employee” means a State or local public
15 safety employee who sustains an injury in the line of duty that:

16 (i) Precludes the individual from continuing to serve or be
17 employed as a State or local public safety employee; and

18 (ii) In the case of a volunteer member of a fire department or
19 ambulance or rescue company or squad, precludes the member from continuing to be
20 employed in the nonpublic safety occupation in which the member is engaged at the
21 time of the injury.

22 (3) “Fund” means the Edward T. Conroy and Jean B. Cryor Scholarship
23 Fund.

24 (4) “School employee” includes an employee of a public or nonpublic
25 school in the State.

26 (5) “State or local public safety employee” means a person who is
27 employed in the State as:

28 (i) A career or volunteer member of a:

29 1. Fire department;

30 2. Ambulance company or squad; or

31 3. Rescue company or squad;

32 (ii) A law enforcement officer;

33 (iii) A correctional officer; or

34 (iv) A member of the Maryland National Guard who was a
35 resident of this State at the time of death.

36 (6) “Surviving spouse” means a person who has not remarried.

37 (7) “Victim of the September 11, 2001, terrorist attacks” means a
38 Maryland resident who was killed as a result of the attacks on the World Trade Center
39 in New York City, the attack on the Pentagon in Virginia, or the crash of United Airlines
40 Flight 93 in Pennsylvania.

41 (b) There is a program of scholarships that are awarded by eligible
42 postsecondary institutions under this section.

43 (c) (1) The program for uniformed services and public safety personnel and
44 their eligible dependents is the Edward T. and Mary A. Conroy Memorial Scholarship
45 Program.

46 (2) The program for eligible dependents of public and nonpublic school
47 employees is the Jean B. Cryor Memorial Scholarship Program.

48 (d) (1) A person may apply to an eligible postsecondary institution for a
49 scholarship under this section if the person:

50 (i) 1. Is accepted for admission or enrolled in the regular
51 undergraduate, graduate or professional program at an eligible institution;

52 2. Is enrolled in a 2–year terminal certificate program in
53 which the course work is acceptable for transfer credit for an accredited baccalaureate
54 program in an eligible institution; or

55 3. Is enrolled in a private career school;

56 (ii) Is at least 16 years old; and

57 (iii) Meets the other eligibility criteria specified in this section.

58 (2) To apply for a scholarship under this section, the following persons
59 are required to be a resident of Maryland at the time of application or at the time of the
60 disabling or fatal event:

61 (i) A son, daughter, stepson, stepdaughter, or the surviving
62 spouse of a member of the uniformed services who:

63 1. Died as a result of service after December 7, 1941;

64 2. Suffered a service connected 100% permanent
65 disability after December 7, 1941; or

66 3. Was declared to be a prisoner of war or missing in
67 action, if that occurred on or after January 1, 1960, as a result of the Vietnam conflict,
68 and if the child was born prior to or while the parent was a prisoner of war or missing
69 in action;

70 (ii) A prisoner of war or missing in action, if that occurred on or
71 after January 1, 1960, as a result of the Vietnam conflict and was a resident of this State
72 at the time the person was declared to be a prisoner of war or missing in action;

73 (iii) A veteran who:

74 1. Suffers a service connected disability of 25% or greater;
75 and

76 2. Has exhausted or is no longer eligible for federal
77 veterans’ educational benefits;

78 (iv) A son, daughter, stepson, or stepdaughter of or the surviving
79 spouse of a victim of the September 11, 2001, terrorist attacks;

80 (v) A son, daughter, stepson, or stepdaughter of a school
81 employee who, as a result of an act of violence:

82 1. Died in the line of duty; or

83 2. Sustained an injury in the line of duty that rendered
84 the school employee 100% disabled; or

85 (vi) The surviving spouse of a school employee who, as a result of
86 an act of violence:

87 1. Died in the line of duty; or

88 2. Sustained an injury in the line of duty that rendered
89 the school employee 100% disabled.

90 (3) To apply for a scholarship under this section, the following persons
91 are not required to be a resident of Maryland at the time of application or at the time of
92 the disabling or fatal event:

93 (i) 1. A son, daughter, stepson, or stepdaughter of any State
94 or local public safety employee killed in the line of duty; or

95 2. The surviving spouse of any State or local public safety
96 employee killed in the line of duty; or

97 (ii) 1. A disabled public safety employee;

98 2. A son, daughter, stepson, or stepdaughter of a disabled
99 public safety employee who sustains an injury in the line of duty that renders the public
100 safety employee 100% disabled; or

101 3. The surviving spouse of a disabled public safety
102 employee who sustains an injury in the line of duty that renders the public safety
103 employee 100% disabled.

104 (e) A scholarship awarded under this section:

105 (1) May be used for the tuition and mandatory fees at any eligible
106 institution; and

107 (2) May not:

108 (i) Exceed the equivalent annual tuition and mandatory fees of
109 a resident undergraduate student at the 4–year public institution of higher education
110 within the University System of Maryland, other than the University of Maryland
111 Global Campus and University of Maryland, Baltimore Campus, with the highest
112 annual expenses for a full–time resident undergraduate; and

113 (ii) Be less than the lesser of:

114 1. $3,000; or

115 2. The equivalent annual tuition and mandatory fees of a
116 resident of the institution attended by the recipient of the scholarship.

117 (f) (1) Each postsecondary institution shall determine the eligibility of
118 persons who apply to the institution for the Edward T. and Mary A. Conroy Memorial
119 Scholarship Program and the Jean B. Cryor Memorial Scholarship Program.

120 (2) Funds for the Edward T. and Mary A. Conroy Memorial Scholarship
121 Program and the Jean B. Cryor Memorial Scholarship Program shall be allocated by the
122 Commission to each postsecondary institution based on [the number of eligible
123 recipients attending each institution] A FORMULA DETERMINED BY THE
124 COMMISSION, WHICH MAY INCLUDE PRIOR YEAR ENROLLMENT, ELIGIBLE
125 RECIPIENTS, AND INSTITUTIONAL TUITION AND FEES.

126 (3) In October and February of each year, each postsecondary
127 institution shall report to the Commission the number of eligible recipients attending
128 the institution.

129 (4) (I) The Commission shall allocate funds for awards to
130 postsecondary institutions [upon verification of eligible recipients attending the
131 institutions] SUBJECT TO THE AVAILABILITY OF FUNDS AS PROVIDED IN THE
132 STATE BUDGET.

133 (II) EACH POSTSECONDARY INSTITUTION MAY MAKE
134 AWARDS TO ELIGIBLE STUDENTS SUBJECT TO THE ALLOCATION OF FUNDS FROM
135 THE COMMISSION UNDER SUBPARAGRAPH (I) OF THIS PARAGRAPH.

136 (5) [If funds cannot be allocated in the fiscal year in which awards are
137 made, priority shall be given to allocating funds for those awards in the following fiscal
138 year] THE COMMISSION SHALL ADOPT GUIDELINES TO IMPLEMENT THIS
139 SUBSECTION.

140 (g) (1) Each recipient of a scholarship under this section may hold the
141 award for 5 years of full–time study or 8 years of part–time study.

142 (2) The number of eligible recipients under subsection (d)(2)(iii) of this
143 section shall be limited to 15 each year.

144 (3) An award provided under subsection (d)(2)(iv) of this section may
145 not exceed the amount specified in subsection (e)(2) of this section when combined with
146 any other scholarship received by a student based on the student’s status as a child or
147 spouse of a victim of the September 11, 2001, terrorist attacks.

148 (4) SUBJECT TO A RECIPIENT’S CONTINUED ELIGIBILITY UNDER
149 SUBSECTION (D) OF THIS SECTION AND THE AVAILABILITY OF FUNDS UNDER
150 SUBSECTION (F) OF THIS SECTION, AN AWARDING AUTHORITY SHALL GIVE
151 PRIORITY TO A RECIPIENT OF A SCHOLARSHIP BEFORE AWARDING FUNDS TO
152 NEW APPLICANTS.

153 (h) (1) There is an Edward T. Conroy and Jean B. Cryor Scholarship Fund.

154 (2) The Commission shall administer the Fund.

155 (3) The Fund is a special, nonlapsing fund that is not subject to § 7–302
156 of the State Finance and Procurement Article.

157 (4) The State Treasurer shall hold the Fund and the Comptroller shall
158 account for the Fund.

159 (5) The Commission:

160 (i) May accept any gift or grant from any person for the Fund;

161 (ii) Shall use any gift or grant that it receives for a scholarship
162 from the programs; and

163 (iii) Shall deposit any gift or grant that it receives for the
164 programs with the State Treasurer.

165 (i) For fiscal year 2024 and each fiscal year thereafter, the Governor shall
166 include in the annual budget bill an appropriation of at least $4,000,000 to the Fund.
