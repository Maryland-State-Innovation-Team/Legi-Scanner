Prince George's County - Expanding Access to Early Childhood Education and 
Child Care 
 
 
This bill requires the Prince George’s County government to develop an implementation 
and financing plan to expand access to high-quality early childhood education and child 
care in Prince George’s County for children from birth through three years of age. The 
county government must consult with the Prince George’s County Public School System, 
the Prince George’s County Council, the Maryland State Department of Education 
(MSDE), businesses, and relevant organizations. The plan must include an analysis of 
(1) the cost to parents, the county, and the State; (2) the workforce needs of early childhood 
education and child care providers to expand access; (3) the capacity of early childhood 
education and child care providers to serve more children; and (4) additional county and 
State revenue options to expand access. By December 1, 2027, the county government must 
submit the plan to the Governor, the General Assembly, and the members of the 
Prince George’s County Delegation to the General Assembly. The bill takes effect  
July 1, 2026. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  MSDE can consult with Prince George’s County with existing resources. 
Revenues are not affected. 
 
Local Effect:  Prince George’s County expenditures may increase by at least 
$200,000 between FY 2027 and 2028, as discussed below. Revenues are not affected. This 
bill may impose a mandate on a unit of local government. 
 
Small Business Effect:  None.

Analysis 
 
Current Law:  The State oversees a variety of early education services that are provided 
to Maryland children and their families in cooperation with local governments, local school 
systems, and private child care providers. The Division of Early Childhood coordinates the 
State’s early childhood care and education system and is composed of three subdivisions:  
(1) the Office of Child Care; (2) the Office of Early Learning and Instruction; and (3) the 
Office of Family Support Services. 
 
Local Expenditures:  Based on a preliminary analysis of the bill by 
Prince Geroge’s County, local expenditures may increase by at least $200,000 for a study. 
The estimate is based on a similar requirement in legislation under consideration by the 
Prince George’s County Council; to the extent the study would be conducted even in the 
absence of the bill because of local legislation, county expenditures directly attributable to 
the bill are likely reduced (or eliminated). 
 
 
Additional Information 
 
Recent Prior Introductions:  Similar legislation has not been introduced within the last 
three years.
