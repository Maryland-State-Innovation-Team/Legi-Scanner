16 [2.] 3.A. The defendant previously has failed to respond to a

18 B. The whereabouts of the defendant are unknown and the
19 issuance of a warrant is necessary to subject the defendant to the jurisdiction of the court;

20 C. The defendant is in custody for another offense; or

21 D. There is probable cause to believe that the defendant poses
22 a danger to another person or to the community.

4 Article – Criminal Law

5 9–503.

6 (a) A person may not make, or cause to be made, a statement or report that
7 the person knows to be false as a whole or in material part to an official or unit of the
8 State or of a county, municipal corporation, or other political subdivision of the State
9 that a crime has been committed or that a condition imminently dangerous to public
10 safety or health exists, with the intent that the official or unit investigate, consider, or
11 take action in connection with that statement or report.

12 (b) A person who violates this section is guilty of a misdemeanor and on
13 conviction is subject to imprisonment not exceeding [6 months] 1 YEAR or a fine not
14 exceeding $500 or both.

15 (C) (1) SUBJECT TO PARAGRAPH (2) OF THIS SUBSECTION, IN
16 ADDITION TO ANY PENALTY IMPOSED UNDER SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION,
17 THE COURT SHALL ORDER A PERSON CONVICTED OR FOUND TO HAVE
18 COMMITTED A VIOLATION OF THIS SECTION TO PAY RESTITUTION IN
19 ACCORDANCE WITH TITLE 11, SUBTITLE 6 OF THE CRIMINAL PROCEDURE
20 ARTICLE.

21 (2) TITLE 11, SUBTITLE 6 OF THE CRIMINAL PROCEDURE
22 ARTICLE MAY NOT BE CONSTRUED TO LIMIT THE AMOUNT OF RESTITUTION THE
23 COURT MAY ORDER UNDER THIS SUBSECTION.
