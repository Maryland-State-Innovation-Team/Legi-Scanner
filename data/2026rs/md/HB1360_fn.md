Maryland Public Ethics Law - Regulated Lobbyists - Reporting Requirements 
 
 
This bill increases the number and frequency of lobbying activity reports that a regulated 
lobbyist must file electronically with the State Ethics Commission (SEC) each year. 
Additionally, the bill requires that the activity report include a list of each matter on which 
the regulated lobbyist performed lobbying activity, including (1) a description of the 
lobbyist’s position on each matter and (2) each bill number or a description of each 
legislative action on which the lobbyist performed lobbying activity.

Analysis 
 
Bill Summary/Current Law:  “Regulated lobbyist” means an entity that is required to 
register with SEC to act as a lobbyist, as specified under the Maryland Public Ethics Law 
(Title 5 of the General Provisions Article). 
 
SEC administers a lobbying regulatory program including registration, disclosure, 
reporting and standards of conduct for executive, legislative and grassroots lobbying. The 
lobbyist standards are comprehensive and address a multitude of issues including 
contingent fees, campaign finance activity, improper encouragement of legislation, 
improper gifts, prohibitions against loans, concealment of clients, referrals by officials or 
employees to obtain lobbying clients, intentional misstatement of fact, and other fraudulent 
or illegal activities. More than 3,000 lobbying registrations are filed each year identifying 
the name of the lobbyist, the employer, and the topics of the activity to be lobbied.  
 
By May 31 and November 30 of each year, a regulated lobbyist must file electronically a 
lobbying activity report with SEC that includes, among other things, specified total 
expenditures in connection with influencing executive or legislative action. The bill 
requires that a regulated lobbyist electronically file two additional activity reports with 
SEC no later than: 
 
 
February 15 each year to cover the period from November 1 of the immediately 
preceding year through January 31 of the current year (both inclusive); and 
 
March 15 of each year to cover the period from February 1 through the last day of 
February of that year (both inclusive). 
 
Lobbyist Registration Fund 
 
The Lobbyist Registration Fund is a special, nonlapsing fund administered by the 
State Treasurer. The fund consists of lobbyist registration fees collected by SEC and may 
be used to defray the expenses of administering the Maryland Public Ethics Law as it 
relates to lobbyist activity. Any balance remaining in the fund at the end of each fiscal year 
reverts to the general fund. 
 
State Expenditures:  To implement the bill, SEC must significantly update/reprogram its 
electronic lobbying reporting system and its lobbyist training system to reflect the bill’s 
changes. The total cost to reprogram both systems is $300,000, which SEC advises will be 
split between general funds and the Lobbyist Registration Fund. Therefore, general and 
special fund expenditures by SEC each increase by $150,000 only in fiscal 2027 for 
contractual IT services to update the agency’s lobbying registration and training systems.
