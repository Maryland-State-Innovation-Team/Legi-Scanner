1 (C) EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SUBSECTION (D) OF THIS SECTION, BETWEEN
2 THE HOURS OF 6 A.M. AND 10 P.M. A MERCHANT MAY NOT:

3 (1) PROHIBIT A PERSON FROM MAKING A CASH PAYMENT TO
4 PURCHASE AN ESSENTIAL CONSUMER GOOD;

5 (2) REQUIRE A PERSON TO USE A CREDIT OR DEBIT CARD TO
6 PURCHASE AN ESSENTIAL CONSUMER GOOD; OR

7 (3) CHARGE OR COLLECT FROM A PERSON A HIGHER PRICE THAN
8 CHARGED OR COLLECTED FOR AN EQUIVALENT CASHLESS TRANSACTION, WHETHER
9 OR NOT THE DIFFERENCE IN PRICE IS DESCRIBED AS A FEE, FOR MAKING A CASH
10 PAYMENT FOR THE PURCHASE OF AN ESSENTIAL CONSUMER GOOD.

11 (D) A MERCHANT MAY PROHIBIT A PERSON FROM MAKING A CASH
12 PAYMENT FOR THE PURCHASE OF A CONSUMER GOOD:

13 (1) IN A CASH DENOMINATION GREATER THAN $20; OR

14 (2) IF THE MERCHANT:

15 (I) PROVIDES AND MAINTAINS A DEVICE ON THE PREMISES
16 OF THE ESTABLISHMENT THAT CONVERTS CASH INTO A PREPAID CARD
17 THAT A PERSON MAY USE TO PURCHASE A CONSUMER GOOD AT THE
18 ESTABLISHMENT;

19 (II) DOES NOT CHARGE ANY FEE OR REQUIRE A DEPOSIT;
20 AND

21 (III) IF THE DEVICE MALFUNCTIONS OR BECOMES
22 INOPERABLE, ACCEPTS PAYMENT IN CASH IN ACCORDANCE WITH THIS SECTION
23 UNTIL THE DEVICE RESUMES NORMAL OPERATIONS.

24 (E) A VIOLATION OF THIS SECTION IS:

25 (1) AN UNFAIR, ABUSIVE, OR DECEPTIVE TRADE PRACTICE WITHIN
26 THE MEANING OF TITLE 13 OF THIS ARTICLE; AND

27 (2) EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SUBSECTION (F) OF THIS SECTION,
28 SUBJECT TO THE ENFORCEMENT AND PENALTY PROVISIONS CONTAINED IN TITLE
29 13 OF THIS ARTICLE.

1 (F) (1) SECTIONS 13–410(A) AND (B) AND 13–411 OF THIS ARTICLE
2 DO NOT APPLY TO A VIOLATION OF THIS SECTION.

3 (2) THE DIVISION OF CONSUMER PROTECTION OF THE OFFICE OF
4 THE ATTORNEY GENERAL SHALL PROVIDE A MERCHANT WHO VIOLATES THIS
