AMENDMENT NO. 1
On page 1, in line 2, strike “Public Financing Act –” and substitute “Campaign
Financing – Campaign Finance Reporting Deadline and”; in the same line, strike
“– Distributions” and substitute “Distributions”; in line 3, after the first “of” insert
“altering the day that a certain campaign finance report is due to be filed with the State
Board of Elections; requiring that a certain campaign finance report cover a certain
period of time;”; in line 5, strike “distributions from the Fair Campaign Financing Fund”
and substitute “campaign finance”; in line 8, after “Section” insert “13–309(a) and”; and
in line 13, after “Section” insert “13–309(b)(2) and (c), 13–312, and”.

AMENDMENT NO. 2

subsection (d) of this section, a campaign finance entity shall file campaign finance
reports as follows:

(2) except for a ballot issue committee, on or before the fifth Tuesday
