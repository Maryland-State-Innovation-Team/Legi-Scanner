Judiciary 
 
 
Vehicle Laws - Eluding Police - Penalties (Zoey's Law) 
 
 
This bill reclassifies a violation of § 21-904(d)(2) of the Transportation Article (attempting 
to elude a police officer resulting in the death of another person) from a misdemeanor to a 
felony. The bill establishes a penalty of imprisonment for up to 5 years and/or a 
$5,000 maximum fine for a first offense and applies the existing criminal penalty 
(imprisonment for up to 10 years and/or a $5,000 maximum fine) to a second or subsequent 
offense.  
   
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  The bill is not anticipated to materially affect State finances or operations.    
  
Local Effect:  The bill is not anticipated to materially affect local finances or operations.   
  
Small Business Effect:  None.  
  
 
Analysis 
 
Current Law:  The driver of a vehicle may not attempt to elude a police officer who gives 
a visual or audible signal to stop by willfully failing to stop the vehicle. This prohibition 
applies when the officer is in uniform (prominently displaying their badge or other insignia 
of office) or operating a vehicle appropriately marked as an official police vehicle (whether 
in uniform or not). A person who violates this prohibition and whose actions result in the 
death of another person is guilty of a misdemeanor and subject to imprisonment for up to 
10 years and/or a maximum fine of $5,000.
