Workgroup for the Deaf, Deafblind, and Hard of Hearing - Extension 
 
 
This bill extends the termination date of the Workgroup for the Deaf, Deafblind, and Hard 
of Hearing from June 30, 2026, to December 31, 2028. By December 1, 2028, the 
workgroup must submit a report of its findings and recommendations to the Governor and 
the General Assembly. The bill takes effect July 1, 2026. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  The bill’s requirements can be handled with existing budgeted resources. 
Revenues are not affected.  
 
Local Effect:  None. 
 
Small Business Effect:  None. 
 
 
Analysis 
 
Current Law:  Chapters 442 and 443 of 2024 established the Workgroup for the Deaf, 
Deafblind, and Hard of Hearing, staffed by Office of the Deaf and Hard of Hearing 
(ODHH), to study and make recommendations regarding services, programs, advocacy, 
outreach, and other efforts to improve the quality of life of individuals who are deaf, 
deafblind, or hard of hearing. More specifically, the workgroup must study, assess, and 
make recommendations regarding:  
 
 
providing direct and specialized case management services for deaf, deafblind, and 
hard of hearing individuals;

As necessary, the workgroup must establish subcommittees and assign members to assist 
in the research and preparation of the workgroup’s report. In May 2025, the workgroup 
published its final report, which noted that more research was necessary to expand on the 
charges of the workgroup that had not been addressed to their fullest extent.  
 
Office of the Deaf and Hard of Hearing 
 
ODHH was established in 2001 to promote the general welfare of deaf and hard of hearing 
individuals in the State. ODHH must fulfill the following duties and responsibilities:   
 
 
provide, advocate, and coordinate the adoption of public policies, regulations, and 
programs that will benefit deaf and hard of hearing individuals;  
 
improve access to communication and to existing services and programs for deaf 
and hard of hearing individuals;  
 
provide direct services to deaf and hard of hearing individuals, as appropriate; 
 
increase public awareness of the needs and issues affecting deaf and hard of hearing 
individuals;  
 
work with State and local agencies to ensure access for deaf and hard of hearing 
individuals to safety and emergency services;  
 
develop a referral service for deaf and hard of hearing individuals;  
 
serve as an information clearinghouse on the needs and issues affecting deaf and 
hard of hearing individuals; 
 
increase access for deaf and hard of hearing individuals to educational, health, and 
social opportunities;

Additional Comments:  The current termination date of the workgroup (June 30, 2026) 
occurs before the effective date of the bill (July 1, 2026). Nevertheless, this analysis 
assumes the workgroup as currently structured is maintained rather than reconstituted.
