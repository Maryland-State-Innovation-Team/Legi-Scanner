2 HOUSE BILL 342

1 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND,
2 That the Laws of Maryland read as follows:

3 Article – Business Occupations and Professions

4 7–304.

5 (a) To qualify for a license, an applicant shall be an individual who meets the
6 requirements of this section.

7 (b) (1) SUBJECT TO PARAGRAPH
8 (2) OF THIS SUBSECTION, AN applicant shall have graduated on completion of a 4–year
9 curriculum in forestry from a college or university that is:

10 (I) approved by the Board [or]; AND

11 (II) accredited by:

12 (I) the Society of American Foresters;. OR

13 (II) AN EQUIVALENT ACCREDITING ENTITY.

14 (2) AN APPLICANT MAY MEET THE REQUIREMENTS OF THIS SUBSECTION IF THE
15 BOARD DETERMINES THAT THE APPLICANT’S EDUCATION, INCLUDING A
16 COMPARABLE GRADUATE DEGREE OR INTERNATIONAL DEGREE, IS
17 SUBSTANTIALLY EQUIVALENT TO THE REQUIREMENTS UNDER PARAGRAPH (1)
18 OF THIS SUBSECTION.

19 (c) The applicant shall have at least 2 years of experience in forestry that
20 indicates to the Board that the applicant is competent to practice forestry.

21 SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That this Act shall take effect
22 October 1, 2026.
