CHAPTER ______

1 AN ACT concerning

2 Maryland Medical Assistance Program and Developmental Disabilities
3 Administration – Home– and Community–Based Services Eligibility
4 Determinations
5 (Maryland Protecting People With Disabilities Act)

6 FOR the privilege of altering the time within which an individual receiving services from
7 the Developmental Disabilities Administration must appeal a loss of eligibility for
8 services; establishing requirements related to eligibility determinations and
9 redeterminations under the Maryland Medical Assistance Program; requiring the
10 Maryland Department of Health to provide certain accessible information to
11 Program recipients and any other individuals on request; prohibiting the
12 Department from procedurally disenrolling individuals from the Program or
13 home– and community–based services solely on a certain basis except under certain
14 circumstances; requiring the Department, subject to federal approval, to reserve
15 certain home– and community–based services waiver slots for Program recipients
16 who lost eligibility under certain circumstances; and generally relating to
17 determinations of eligibility for home– and community–based services.

27 Preamble

28 WHEREAS, The landmark decision of the U.S. Supreme Court in Olmstead v. L.C.
29 ex rel. Zimring, 527 U.S. 581 (1999) affirmed that unjustified segregation of individuals
30 with disabilities constitutes discrimination in violation of Title II of the federal Americans
31 with Disabilities Act; and

32 WHEREAS, The Olmstead decision established that people with disabilities have
33 the right to receive services in the most integrated setting appropriate to their needs and
34 that states must provide community–based services when such services are appropriate,
35 desired by the individual, and can be reasonably accommodated; and

36 WHEREAS, Individuals with disabilities are vital members of Maryland’s communities,
37 contributing meaningfully in workplaces, classrooms, places of worship, neighborhoods,
38 and civic life across the State; and

39 WHEREAS, The State of Maryland has demonstrated a longstanding commitment
40 to community inclusion by investing in supports that allow Marylanders with
41 disabilities to live, work, and thrive in the communities of their choosing, with people
42 they choose; and

43 WHEREAS, Ensuring timely Medicaid eligibility determinations, preventing
44 procedural terminations, and protecting continuity of home– and community–based
45 services coverage are necessary to fulfill the promise of Olmstead and uphold Maryland’s
46 commitment to dignity, inclusion, and equality for all people with disabilities; and

47 WHEREAS, Maryland law requires the Developmental Disabilities Administration
48 and the Department of Disabilities to compile statewide and regional data from written
49 plans of habilitation, including barriers to community placement, and to incorporate the
50 data annually into the State’s Olmstead Plan together with recommendations for
51 addressing those barriers; now, therefore,

70 (a) In this title the following words have the meanings indicated.

71 (h) “Program” means the Maryland Medical Assistance Program.

72 (i) “Program recipient” means an individual who receives benefits under the

74 15–103.9.

75 (A) (1) IN THIS SECTION THE FOLLOWING WORDS HAVE THE MEANINGS
76 INDICATED.

77 (2) “EX PARTE REDETERMINATION” MEANS A PROCESS BY WHICH
78 THE DEPARTMENT DETERMINES A PROGRAM RECIPIENT’S ELIGIBILITY TO
79 CONTINUE TO RECEIVE SERVICES USING EXISTING DATA, WITHOUT REQUIRING THE
80 PROGRAM RECIPIENT TO SUBMIT ADDITIONAL INFORMATION.

81 (3) “HOME– AND COMMUNITY–BASED SERVICES” MEANS SERVICES
82 AUTHORIZED UNDER A MEDICAID STATE PLAN OPTION OR WAIVER UNDER § 1115
83 OR § 1915(C), (I), OR (K) OF THE FEDERAL SOCIAL SECURITY ACT.

84 (4) “PROCEDURAL DISENROLLMENT” MEANS THE TERMINATION OF A
85 PROGRAM RECIPIENT’S ELIGIBILITY TO RECEIVE SERVICES BASED ON REASONS
86 RELATED TO THE RENEWAL PROCESS AND NOT ON A DETERMINATION OF
87 INELIGIBILITY.

88 (B) THE DEPARTMENT SHALL COMPLY WITH THE REQUIREMENTS OF 42
89 C.F.R. § 435.912 RELATED TO THE TIMELY DETERMINATION AND
90 REDETERMINATION OF PROGRAM ELIGIBILITY, INCLUDING PROCESSING AN
91 APPLICATION:

92 (1) IF A DETERMINATION OF DISABILITY IS NOT REQUIRED, WITHIN
93 45 DAYS AFTER THE RECEIPT OF THE APPLICATION; AND

94 (2) IF A DETERMINATION OF DISABILITY IS REQUIRED, WITHIN 90
95 DAYS AFTER THE RECEIPT OF THE APPLICATION.

96 (C) THE DEPARTMENT SHALL CONDUCT EX PARTE REDETERMINATIONS
97 AND USE ALL PROCEDURES AUTHORIZED UNDER 42 C.F.R. § 435.916 TO PREVENT
98 PROCEDURAL DISENROLLMENT OF INDIVIDUALS RECEIVING HOME– AND
99 COMMUNITY–BASED SERVICES.

100 (D) IN ACCORDANCE WITH 42 C.F.R. § 435.905 AND SUBJECT TO
101 SUBSECTION (E) OF THIS SECTION, THE DEPARTMENT SHALL PROVIDE, IN
102 ELECTRONIC AND PAPER FORMATS, AND ORALLY AS AN ACCOMMODATION, THE
103 FOLLOWING INFORMATION TO ALL APPLICANTS AND OTHER INDIVIDUALS ON
104 REQUEST:

105 (1) REQUIREMENTS FOR PROGRAM ELIGIBILITY;

106 (2) AVAILABLE PROGRAM SERVICES; AND

107 (3) THE RIGHTS AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF APPLICANTS AND
108 PROGRAM RECIPIENTS.

109 (E) (1) THE DEPARTMENT SHALL PROVIDE THE INFORMATION
110 REQUIRED UNDER SUBSECTION (D) OF THIS SECTION IN PLAIN LANGUAGE AND IN A
111 MANNER THAT IS TIMELY AND ACCESSIBLE TO INDIVIDUALS WHO ARE:

112 (I) LIMITED ENGLISH PROFICIENT THROUGH THE PROVISION
113 OF LANGUAGE SERVICES, INCLUDING ORAL INTERPRETATION AND WRITTEN
114 TRANSLATIONS, AT NO COST TO THE INDIVIDUAL; OR

115 (II) LIVING WITH DISABILITIES THROUGH THE PROVISION OF
116 AUXILIARY AIDS AND SERVICES AT NO COST TO THE INDIVIDUAL IN ACCORDANCE
117 WITH THE FEDERAL AMERICANS WITH DISABILITIES ACT AND § 504 OF THE
118 FEDERAL REHABILITATION ACT OF 1973.

119 (2) THE DEPARTMENT SHALL PROVIDE TAGLINES IN NON–ENGLISH
120 LANGUAGES TO INFORM INDIVIDUALS OF:

121 (I) THE AVAILABILITY OF THE ACCESSIBLE INFORMATION AND
122 LANGUAGE SERVICES UNDER THIS SECTION; AND

123 (II) HOW TO ACCESS THE INFORMATION AND SERVICES.

124 (F) (1) IF A PROGRAM RECIPIENT IS PROCEDURALLY DISENROLLED
125 FROM HOME– AND COMMUNITY–BASED SERVICES DUE TO A FAILURE BY THE
126 DEPARTMENT TO REDETERMINE ELIGIBILITY IN A TIMELY MANNER AFTER THE
127 RECIPIENT HAS TIMELY RETURNED INFORMATION REQUIRED UNDER 42 C.F.R §
128 435.916(B)(2)(1)(B) AND 42 C.F.R § 435.952(C), THE DEPARTMENT SHALL
129 REINSTATE THE PROGRAM RECIPIENT’S ELIGIBILITY AND THE PROVISION OF
130 HOME– AND COMMUNITY–BASED SERVICES AND AUTHORIZE ENROLLMENT
131 RETROACTIVELY TO THE DATE OF DISENROLLMENT PENDING THE DEPARTMENT’S
132 COMPLETION OF THE REDETERMINATION PROCESS.

133 (2) THIS SUBSECTION MAY NOT BE CONSTRUED TO LIMIT THE
134 DEPARTMENT’S AUTHORITY OR DIMINISH THE DEPARTMENT’S RESPONSIBILITY TO
135 REINSTATE THE ELIGIBILITY OF AND THE PROVISION OF HOME– AND
136 COMMUNITY–BASED SERVICES TO A PROGRAM RECIPIENT WHO HAS BEEN
137 DISENROLLED FOR A REASON OTHER THAN A FAILURE BY THE DEPARTMENT TO
138 REDETERMINE ELIGIBILITY IN A TIMELY MANNER.

139 (G) SUBJECT TO FEDERAL APPROVAL, THE DEPARTMENT SHALL RESERVE
140 CAPACITY IN THE HOME– AND COMMUNITY–BASED SERVICES WAIVERS FOR
141 SERVICES NEEDED BY PROGRAM RECIPIENTS WITH DEVELOPMENTAL
142 DISABILITIES WHO:

143 (1) WERE DISENROLLED FROM THE MARYLAND MEDICAID HOME
144 AND COMMUNITY–BASED SERVICES WAIVER PROGRAM ON OR AFTER JANUARY 1,
145 2024;

146 (2) HAVE HAD PROGRAM ELIGIBILITY REINSTATED; AND

147 (3) HAVE REQUESTED THE REINSTATEMENT OF WAIVER SERVICES.

148 (H) (1) THE DEPARTMENT MAY NOT PROCEDURALLY DISENROLL
149 AN INDIVIDUAL FROM THE PROGRAM OR HOME– AND COMMUNITY–BASED SERVICES
150 SOLELY BASED ON MISSING DOCUMENTATION, A MISSING SIGNATURE, OR
151 INCOMPLETE INFORMATION UNLESS THE DEPARTMENT HAS:

152 (I) EXHAUSTED ALL EX PARTE VERIFICATION PROCESSES
153 REQUIRED UNDER 42 C.F.R. § 435.916;

154 (II) PROVIDED A CLEAR, SPECIFIC, AND ACCESSIBLE WRITTEN
155 NOTICE IDENTIFYING THE EXACT INFORMATION REQUIRED; AND

156 (III) PROVIDED THE PROGRAM RECIPIENT WITH A REASONABLE
157 OPPORTUNITY TO SUPPLY THE INFORMATION.

158 (2) IF THE DEPARTMENT VIOLATES PARAGRAPH (1) OF THIS
159 SUBSECTION, A PROGRAM RECIPIENT’S HOME– AND COMMUNITY–BASED SERVICES
160 SHALL CONTINUE WITHOUT INTERRUPTION.

161 (I) IF THE DEPARTMENT DISENROLLS A PROGRAM RECIPIENT IN
162 VIOLATION OF THIS SECTION, THE DEPARTMENT SHALL:

163 (1) AUTOMATICALLY REINSTATE PROGRAM AND HOME– AND
164 COMMUNITY–BASED SERVICES RETROACTIVE TO THE DATE OF DISENROLLMENT;
165 AND

166 (2) TREAT THE PROGRAM RECIPIENT AS CONTINUOUSLY ENROLLED.
