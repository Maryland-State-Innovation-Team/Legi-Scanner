(ii) subject to paragraph (2) of this subsection, the administrative

individual did not commit or suborn perjury, fabricate evidence, or by the individual’s
own conduct cause or bring about the conviction.

(2) (i) In determining the weight and admissibility of evidence
presented by the parties, the administrative law judge may, in the interest of justice,
give due consideration to the passage of time, death or unavailability of witnesses, the
destruction of evidence, or any other factor.
