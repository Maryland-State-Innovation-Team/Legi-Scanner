Baltimore City - 40th Alcoholic Beverages District - Authorizations and 
Revisions 
 
 
This bill authorizes the Baltimore City Board of License Commissioners to issue, in the 
40th alcoholic beverages district, (1) a Class B beer, wine, and liquor license for a 
restaurant on the odd side of the 1000 block of West Baltimore Street if the applicant 
executes a memorandum of understanding with the Hollins Roundhouse Association, the 
applicant does not transfer the license to a different location or to another person and the 
board waives the minimum seating requirement of 75 individuals and (2) a Class B beer, 
wine, and liquor license for a restaurant on the even side of the 800 block of  
North Charles Street if the applicant executes a memorandum of understanding with the 
Mount Vernon-Belvedere Association and the board waives the minimum seating 
requirement of 75 individuals. The bill specifies that a Class C beer, wine, and liquor 
license in the 1200 block of Eutaw Place may be issued if the board waives any 
requirements that sales may be made only to members and guests of members. The bill 
also alters the boundary in which license holders in a specified area of the 40th alcoholic 
beverages district are authorized to sell alcoholic beverages from 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. The 
bill takes effect July 1, 2026. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  None. 
 
Local Effect:  The bill does not materially affect Baltimore City finances or operations. 
 
Small Business Effect:  Minimal.

Analysis 
 
Current Law:  Generally, the board may not issue a new alcoholic beverages license in 
the 40th alcoholic beverages district. However, the board may issue a Class B beer, wine, 
and liquor license to a restaurant in the 40th alcoholic beverages district if the restaurant  
(1) has a minimum capital investment, not including the cost of land and buildings, of 
$200,000 for restaurant facilities and (2) has a minimum seating capacity of 75 individuals. 
The annual license fee is $2,060. 
 
The board may issue a Class C beer, wine, and liquor license in the 1200 block of 
Eutaw Place in the 40th alcoholic beverages district if the applicant executes a 
memorandum of understanding with the Bolton Hill Community Association. The annual 
license fee is $860.
