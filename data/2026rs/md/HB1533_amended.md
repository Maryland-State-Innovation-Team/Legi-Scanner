CHAPTER ______

1 AN ACT concerning

2 Public Health – Cosmetic Products – Enforcement and Penalties for Prohibited
3 Ingredients
4 (Crown and Care Act – Protecting Communities From Harmful Hair Chemicals)

5 making a violation of certain
6 provisions of law regarding the manufacture, sale,
7 delivery, holding, or offering for sale certain cosmetic products an unfair, abusive, or
8 deceptive trade practice; providing that a person who violates the prohibition on the
9 manufacture, sale, delivery, holding, or offering for sale a cosmetic product that
10 contains certain ingredients is liable for certain civil damages resulting from actual
11 harm sustained by an individual; authorizing the Maryland Department of Health
12 to investigate a complaint alleging that a person included certain prohibited
13 ingredients in cosmetic products; and generally relating to
14 prohibited ingredients in
15 cosmetic products.

1 (D) (1) IN ADDITION TO ANY OTHER PENALTIES UNDER THIS TITLE, A
2 PERSON WHO VIOLATES THIS SECTION IS LIABLE FOR CIVIL DAMAGES RESULTING
3 FROM ACTUAL HARM SUSTAINED BY THE INDIVIDUAL RESULTING FROM THE
4 VIOLATION.

5 (2) AN ACTION UNDER THIS SECTION MUST BE BROUGHT WITHIN 3
6 YEARS AFTER THE DATE ON WHICH THE PLAINTIFF DISCOVERED OR REASONABLY
7 SHOULD HAVE DISCOVERED THAT THE EXPOSURE TO AN INGREDIENT IN VIOLATION
8 OF THIS SECTION WAS THE PROXIMATE CAUSE OF THE INJURY SUSTAINED BY THE
9 PLAINTIFF.

10 (3) IN ANY ACTION BROUGHT TO ENFORCE THIS SECTION, A COURT
11 MAY AWARD REASONABLE ATTORNEY’S FEES TO A PREVAILING PLAINTIFF:

12 (I) TREBLE DAMAGES;

13 (II) IF THE VIOLATION WAS WILLFUL, PUNITIVE DAMAGES; AND

14 (III) REASONABLE ATTORNEY’S FEES.

15 (E) (1) THE DEPARTMENT MAY INVESTIGATE ANY COMPLAINT ALLEGING
16 THAT A PERSON HAS VIOLATED THIS SECTION.

17 (2) ON RECEIPT OF A COMPLAINT, A REPRESENTATIVE OF THE
18 DEPARTMENT, AT A REASONABLE TIME, MAY ENTER AND INSPECT THE PREMISES OF
19 THE PERSON LOCATED IN THE STATE TO DETERMINE COMPLIANCE WITH THIS
20 SECTION.

21 (3) THE PERSON MAY NOT:

22 (I) REFUSE TO GRANT ACCESS TO A REPRESENTATIVE WHO
23 REQUESTS TO ENTER AND INSPECT THE PREMISES OF THE PERSON UNDER
24 PARAGRAPH (2) OF THIS SUBSECTION; OR

25 (II) INTERFERE WITH AN INSPECTION UNDER PARAGRAPH (2)
26 OF THIS SUBSECTION.

27 (4) AN INVESTIGATION OF A PREMISES OF A PERSON CONDUCTED
28 UNDER THIS SUBSECTION MAY INCLUDE SAMPLING OF A COSMETIC PRODUCT TO
29 DETERMINE IF THE COSMETIC PRODUCT CONTAINS A TECHNICALLY UNAVOIDABLE
30 TRACE QUANTITY OF AN INGREDIENT LISTED IN SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION.

5 uterine cancer, hair loss, and related conditions caused by harmful hair chemicals that

7 (i) all funds, excluding funds received by the aggrieved party and
8 the costs of the action the Attorney General is entitled to recover, received by the

10 21–259.2 of the Health – General Article under the Consumer Protection Act; and

11 (ii) interest earnings of the fund.

12 (2) The plan developed under paragraph (1) of this subsection shall
13 include the best method of using the money in the fund to achieve the purpose of the
14 special fund.

15 (b) On or before December 1, 2026, the Maryland Department of Health, in
16 conjunction with the Office of the Attorney General, shall report the plan developed
17 under subsection (a) of this section to the Senate Finance Committee and the House
18 Health Committee, in accordance with § 2-1257 of the State Government Article.
