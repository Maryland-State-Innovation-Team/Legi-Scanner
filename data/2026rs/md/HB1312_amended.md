8 SUBTITLE 13. SPECIE AS LEGAL TENDER.

9 12–1301.

10 (A) IN THIS SUBTITLE THE FOLLOWING WORDS HAVE THE MEANINGS
11 INDICATED.

12 (B) (1) “LEGAL TENDER” MEANS A MEDIUM OF EXCHANGE RECOGNIZED
13 BY THE STATE FOR THE PAYMENT OF DEBTS, TAXES, FEES, AND OTHER
14 OBLIGATIONS.

15 (2) “LEGAL TENDER” INCLUDES SPECIE.

16 (C) “SPECIE” MEANS:

17 (1) GOLD OR SILVER COIN ISSUED BY THE UNITED STATES OR A
18 FOREIGN GOVERNMENT; OR

19 (2) GOLD OR SILVER IN THE FORM OF BARS OR OTHER PHYSICAL
20 FORM CERTIFIED AS AT LEAST 99% PURE.

21 12–1302.

22 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY FINDS THAT:

23 (1) THE USE OF SPECIE AS A MEDIUM OF EXCHANGE HAS A
24 HISTORICAL BASIS IN FOSTERING ECONOMIC STABILITY;

25 (2) RECOGNIZING SPECIE AS LEGAL TENDER PROMOTES
26 COMPETITION IN THE MONETARY SYSTEM AND PROTECTS AGAINST RELIANCE ON
27 FIAT CURRENCY; AND

28 (3) ESTABLISHING MECHANISMS FOR THE USE OF PRECIOUS METAL
29 IN TRANSACTIONS ENHANCES ECONOMIC RESILIENCE IN THE STATE.

30 12–1303.

31 (A) SUBJECT TO SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION, A PERSON MAY USE
32 SPECIE FOR THE PAYMENT OF:

33 (1) DEBTS BETWEEN PRIVATE PARTIES, IF THE PARTIES MUTUALLY
34 AGREE TO USE SPECIE; AND

35 (2) TAXES, FEES, OR OTHER OBLIGATIONS OWED TO THE STATE OR A
36 POLITICAL SUBDIVISION, IF THE STATE OR POLITICAL SUBDIVISION AGREES TO
37 ACCEPT SPECIE AS PAYMENT.
