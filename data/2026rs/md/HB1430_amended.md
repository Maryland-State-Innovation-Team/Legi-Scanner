2. A PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL MAY RECEIVE FUNDS

14 (II) FUNDS DISBURSED UNDER SUBPARAGRAPH (I) OF THIS
15 PARAGRAPH SHALL BE PAID DIRECTLY TO THE PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL AND MAY
16 NOT BE PAID TO A LOCAL GOVERNING BODY OR COUNTY BOARD.

(3) IF A PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL IS LOCATED IN A BUILDING
OWNED BY THE LOCAL SCHOOL SYSTEM, THE INTERAGENCY COMMISSION ON
SCHOOL CONSTRUCTION MAY NOT DISTRIBUTE FUNDS TO THE PUBLIC CHARTER
SCHOOL FOR CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS TO THE SCHOOL FACILITY, DEBT
SERVICE, OR RENT OR LEASE PAYMENTS UNDER THIS SUBSECTION IN FISCAL
YEARS DURING WHICH THE SCHOOL FACILITY IS UNDERGOING A PROJECT THAT
IS RECEIVING STATE FUNDING THROUGH THE CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT
PROGRAM.

(4) IF THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF THE CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT
PROGRAM IN A FISCAL YEAR IS LESS THAN THE TOTAL AMOUNT IN THE PRIOR
FISCAL YEAR, THE AMOUNT OF FUNDS DISTRIBUTED TO PUBLIC CHARTER
SCHOOLS UNDER THIS SUBSECTION FOR THE FISCAL YEAR SHALL BE
PROPORTIONATELY REDUCED.

(5) THE INTERAGENCY COMMISSION ON SCHOOL CONSTRUCTION
MAY NOT USE FUNDS DISTRIBUTED TO A PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL UNDER THIS
SUBSECTION AS JUSTIFICATION TO REDUCE THE AMOUNT PROVIDED TO A LOCAL
SCHOOL SYSTEM THROUGH THE CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PROGRAM.

(6) A PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL THAT RECEIVES FUNDS UNDER
THIS SUBSECTION DURING A FISCAL YEAR SHALL REPORT TO THE INTERAGENCY
COMMISSION ON SCHOOL CONSTRUCTION ON THE STATUS OF ANY PROJECT FOR
CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS TO PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL FACILITIES, DEBT
SERVICE, OR RENT OR LEASE PAYMENTS BEFORE THE PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL
MAY RECEIVE FUNDS FOR THE FOLLOWING FISCAL YEAR.

17 (7) FOR FISCAL YEAR 2028 AND EACH FISCAL YEAR THEREAFTER,
18 THE GOVERNOR SHALL INCLUDE IN THE ANNUAL BUDGET BILL AN APPROPRIATION
19 FOR CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS TO PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL FACILITIES, DEBT SERVICE, OR RENT OR LEASE PAYMENTS
20 THAT IS EQUAL TO THE TOTAL AGGREGATE STUDENT ENROLLMENT IN THE
21 MARYLAND PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL PROGRAM FOR THE PRIOR FISCAL YEAR
22 MULTIPLIED BY $1,600 $200.

23 (8) (I) THE INTERAGENCY COMMISSION ON SCHOOL CONSTRUCTION
24 SHALL ADOPT REGULATIONS TO CARRY OUT THIS SUBSECTION.

(II) IN THE REGULATIONS ADOPTED UNDER SUBPARAGRAPH
(I) OF THIS PARAGRAPH, THE INTERAGENCY COMMISSION ON SCHOOL
CONSTRUCTION SHALL INCLUDE REGULATIONS THAT:

1. ESTABLISH THE PROCEDURES TO COMPLY WITH
PARAGRAPH (3) OF THIS SUBSECTION IF A LOCAL SCHOOL SYSTEM SUBMITS A
MID–YEAR CAPITAL PROJECT FUNDING REQUEST; AND

2. ENSURE A PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL RECEIVES
FUNDS UNDER THIS SUBSECTION IF THE LOCAL SCHOOL SYSTEM RESCINDS A
CAPITAL PROJECT FUNDING REQUEST AND THE PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL IS
SUBSEQUENTLY ELIGIBLE FOR FUNDING UNDER THIS SUBSECTION.

25 SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That this Act shall take effect July
