1 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND,
2 That the Laws of Maryland read as follows:

3 Article – Education

4 9–109.

5 (C) (1) IN THIS SUBSECTION, “PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL FACILITIES” MEANS
6 PREMISES THAT ARE:

7 (I) OWNED OR LEASED BY A PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL;
8 OR

9 (II) OCCUPIED AND UTILIZED BY A PUBLIC CHARTER
10 SCHOOL TO PROVIDE A PROGRAM OF PRIMARY OR SECONDARY EDUCATION TO ITS
11 STUDENTS.

12 (2) (I) 1. SUBJECT TO SUBSUBPARAGRAPH 2 OF THIS
13 SUBPARAGRAPH, THE INTERAGENCY COMMISSION ON SCHOOL CONSTRUCTION
14 SHALL DISTRIBUTE FUNDS TO PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOLS IN ACCORDANCE WITH
15 THIS SUBSECTION FOR CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS TO PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL
16 FACILITIES, DEBT SERVICE, OR RENT OR LEASE PAYMENTS.

17 2. A PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL MAY RECEIVE FUNDS
18 UNDER THIS SUBSECTION ONLY IF THE PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL HAD AN
19 UNQUALIFIED AUDIT IN THE PRIOR FISCAL YEAR.

20 (II) FUNDS DISBURSED UNDER SUBPARAGRAPH (I) OF THIS
21 PARAGRAPH SHALL BE PAID DIRECTLY TO THE PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL AND MAY
22 NOT BE PAID TO A LOCAL GOVERNING BODY OR COUNTY BOARD.

1 (3) IF A PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL IS LOCATED IN A BUILDING OWNED
2 BY THE LOCAL SCHOOL SYSTEM, THE INTERAGENCY COMMISSION ON SCHOOL
3 CONSTRUCTION MAY NOT DISTRIBUTE FUNDS TO THE PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL
4 FOR CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS TO THE SCHOOL FACILITY, DEBT SERVICE, OR RENT
5 OR LEASE PAYMENTS UNDER THIS SUBSECTION IN FISCAL YEARS DURING WHICH THE
6 SCHOOL FACILITY IS UNDERGOING A PROJECT THAT IS RECEIVING STATE FUNDING
7 THROUGH THE CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PROGRAM.

8 (4) IF THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF THE CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PROGRAM
9 IN A FISCAL YEAR IS LESS THAN THE TOTAL AMOUNT IN THE PRIOR FISCAL YEAR, THE
10 AMOUNT OF FUNDS DISTRIBUTED TO PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOLS UNDER THIS
11 SUBSECTION FOR THE FISCAL YEAR SHALL BE PROPORTIONATELY REDUCED.

12 (5) THE INTERAGENCY COMMISSION ON SCHOOL CONSTRUCTION
13 MAY NOT USE FUNDS DISTRIBUTED TO A PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL UNDER THIS
14 SUBSECTION AS JUSTIFICATION TO REDUCE THE AMOUNT PROVIDED TO A LOCAL
15 SCHOOL SYSTEM THROUGH THE CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PROGRAM.

16 (6) A PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL THAT RECEIVES FUNDS UNDER THIS
17 SUBSECTION DURING A FISCAL YEAR SHALL REPORT TO THE INTERAGENCY
18 COMMISSION ON SCHOOL CONSTRUCTION ON THE STATUS OF ANY PROJECT FOR
19 CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS TO PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL FACILITIES, DEBT SERVICE,
20 OR RENT OR LEASE PAYMENTS BEFORE THE PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL MAY RECEIVE
21 FUNDS FOR THE FOLLOWING FISCAL YEAR.

22 (7) FOR FISCAL YEAR 2028 AND EACH FISCAL YEAR
23 THEREAFTER, THE GOVERNOR SHALL INCLUDE IN THE ANNUAL BUDGET BILL AN
24 APPROPRIATION FOR CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS TO PUBLIC CHARTER SCHOOL 
25 FACILITIES, DEBT SERVICE, OR RENT OR LEASE PAYMENTS THAT IS EQUAL TO THE 
26 TOTAL AGGREGATE STUDENT ENROLLMENT IN THE MARYLAND PUBLIC CHARTER 
27 SCHOOL PROGRAM FOR THE PRIOR FISCAL YEAR MULTIPLIED BY $1,600 $200.

28 (8) (I) THE INTERAGENCY COMMISSION ON SCHOOL
29 CONSTRUCTION SHALL ADOPT REGULATIONS TO CARRY OUT THIS SUBSECTION.

30 (II) IN THE REGULATIONS ADOPTED UNDER SUBPARAGRAPH (I)
31 OF THIS PARAGRAPH, THE INTERAGENCY COMMISSION ON SCHOOL CONSTRUCTION
32 SHALL INCLUDE REGULATIONS THAT:

33 1. ESTABLISH THE PROCEDURES TO COMPLY WITH
34 PARAGRAPH (3) OF THIS SUBSECTION IF A LOCAL SCHOOL SYSTEM SUBMITS A
35 MID–YEAR CAPITAL PROJECT FUNDING REQUEST; AND
