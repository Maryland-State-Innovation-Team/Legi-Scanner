1 Article – Correctional Services

2 1–101.

3 (a) In this article the following words have the meanings indicated.

4 (j) “Immigration detention facility” means any building, facility, or structure
5 used, in whole or in part, to house or detain individuals for federal civil immigration
6 violations.

7 1–103.

8 (A) (1) IN THIS SECTION THE FOLLOWING WORDS HAVE THE
9 MEANINGS INDICATED.

10 (2) (I) “DETENTION FACILITY” MEANS ANY BUILDING,
11 FACILITY, OR STRUCTURE USED, IN WHOLE OR IN PART, TO HOUSE OR DETAIN
12 INDIVIDUALS FOR CIVIL OR CRIMINAL VIOLATIONS.

13 (II) “DETENTION FACILITY” INCLUDES AN IMMIGRATION
14 DETENTION FACILITY.

15 (III) “DETENTION FACILITY” DOES NOT INCLUDE A FACILITY:

16 1. A. THAT IS A HEALTH CARE FACILITY LICENSED
17 UNDER TITLE 19 OF THE HEALTH – GENERAL ARTICLE; OR

18 B. THAT IS A RESIDENTIAL CHILD CARE PROGRAM OR
19 CHILD PLACEMENT AGENCY LICENSED UNDER TITLE 5, SUBTITLE 5 OF THE
20 FAMILY LAW ARTICLE; AND

21 2. WHOSE PRIMARY PURPOSE IS TO PROVIDE
22 HEALTH CARE, TREATMENT, EDUCATION, OR REHABILITATION SERVICES.

23 (3) “UNIT OF LOCAL GOVERNMENT” MEANS A COUNTY, A
24 MUNICIPALITY, OR THE MARYLAND–NATIONAL CAPITAL PARK AND PLANNING
25 COMMISSION.

26 (B) NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER PROVISION OF LAW, THE STATE OR
27 A UNIT OF LOCAL GOVERNMENT MAY NOT APPROVE THE CONSTRUCTION OR
28 OPERATION OF A BUILDING, STRUCTURE, OR OTHER REAL PROPERTY TO BE
29 USED BY A PRIVATE ENTITY AS A DETENTION FACILITY.

30 (C) A PRIVATE ENTITY MAY NOT OPERATE OR OCCUPY A BUILDING,
31 STRUCTURE, OR OTHER REAL PROPERTY AS A DETENTION FACILITY.

32 (D) FOR PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION, A BUILDING, STRUCTURE, OR
33 OTHER REAL PROPERTY SHALL BE CONSIDERED PROPOSED FOR USE AS A
34 DETENTION FACILITY, REGARDLESS OF HOW THE USE IS DESCRIBED IN AN
35 APPLICATION OR PERMIT REQUEST, IF:

36 (1) INDIVIDUALS ARE INTENDED TO BE HELD IN INVOLUNTARY
37 CUSTODY AND WILL NOT BE FREE TO LEAVE; OR

38 (2) THE DESIGN, CONSTRUCTION, OR IMPROVEMENTS INCLUDE
39 FEATURES CONSISTENT WITH SECURE OR INVOLUNTARY CONFINEMENT,
40 INCLUDING:

41 (I) PERIMETER SECURITY MEASURES DESIGNED TO
42 PREVENT OCCUPANTS FROM LEAVING;

43 (II) CONTROLLED ACCESS POINTS; OR

44 (III) LOCKED HOLDING AREAS.

45 (E) (1) THE ATTORNEY GENERAL MAY BRING A CIVIL ACTION TO
46 ENFORCE THIS SECTION.

47 (2) THE COURT MAY ISSUE TEMPORARY, PRELIMINARY, OR
48 PERMANENT INJUNCTIVE RELIEF TO PREVENT AN ACT THAT WOULD CONSTITUTE
49 A VIOLATION OF THIS SECTION.

50 (F) A PRIVATE ENTITY THAT OPERATES OR ATTEMPTS TO OPERATE A
51 DETENTION FACILITY IN VIOLATION OF THIS SECTION IS SUBJECT TO A CIVIL
52 PENALTY NOT EXCEEDING $10,000 FOR EACH DAY THE VIOLATION CONTINUES.

53 (G) THE REMEDIES PROVIDED UNDER THIS SECTION ARE IN ADDITION
54 TO ANY OTHER REMEDY AVAILABLE UNDER STATE OR LOCAL LAW.

57 Article – Correctional Services

58 1–103.

59 (A) (1) IN THIS SECTION THE FOLLOWING WORDS HAVE THE MEANINGS
60 INDICATED.

61 (2) (I) “DETENTION FACILITY” MEANS ANY BUILDING,
62 FACILITY, OR STRUCTURE USED, IN WHOLE OR IN PART, TO HOUSE OR DETAIN
63 INDIVIDUALS FOR CIVIL OR CRIMINAL VIOLATIONS.

64 (II) “DETENTION FACILITY” INCLUDES AN IMMIGRATION
65 DETENTION FACILITY.

66 (III) “DETENTION FACILITY” DOES NOT INCLUDE A FACILITY:

67 1. A. THAT IS A HEALTH CARE FACILITY LICENSED
68 UNDER TITLE 19 OF THE HEALTH – GENERAL ARTICLE; OR

69 B. THAT IS A RESIDENTIAL CHILD CARE PROGRAM OR
70 CHILD PLACEMENT AGENCY LICENSED UNDER TITLE 5, SUBTITLE 5 OF THE
71 FAMILY LAW ARTICLE; AND

72 2. WHOSE PRIMARY PURPOSE IS TO PROVIDE HEALTH
73 CARE, TREATMENT, EDUCATION, OR REHABILITATION SERVICES.

74 (3) “UNIT OF LOCAL GOVERNMENT” MEANS A COUNTY, A
75 MUNICIPALITY, OR THE MARYLAND–NATIONAL CAPITAL PARK AND PLANNING
76 COMMISSION.

77 (B) (1) NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER PROVISION OF LAW, THE STATE
78 OR A UNIT OF LOCAL GOVERNMENT MAY NOT APPROVE THE CONSTRUCTION OR
79 OPERATION OF A BUILDING, STRUCTURE, OR OTHER REAL PROPERTY TO BE
80 USED BY A PRIVATE ENTITY FOR A DETENTION FACILITY UNLESS THE
81 BUILDING, STRUCTURE, OR OTHER REAL PROPERTY IS LOCATED IN A
82 ZONE THAT EXPRESSLY AUTHORIZES IMMIGRATION DETENTION
83 FACILITIES.

84 (2) A GENERAL ZONING CLASSIFICATION AUTHORIZING
85 GOVERNMENT, PUBLIC, OR INSTITUTIONAL USES DOES NOT CONSTITUTE EXPRESS
86 AUTHORIZATION FOR A BUILDING, STRUCTURE, OR OTHER REAL PROPERTY TO
87 BE USED AS A DETENTION FACILITY BY A PRIVATE ENTITY.

88 (3) BEFORE ESTABLISHING A ZONING CLASSIFICATION
89 AUTHORIZING PRIVATE DETENTION FACILITIES, THE STATE OR A UNIT OF LOCAL
90 GOVERNMENT SHALL:

91 (I) CONSIDER:

92 1. CONSISTENCY WITH ANY COMPREHENSIVE PLAN
93 FOR THE SURROUNDING AREA;

94 2. COMPATIBILITY WITH SURROUNDING LAND USES;
95 AND

96 3. THE ABILITY OF LOCAL INFRASTRUCTURE TO
97 ADEQUATELY SUPPORT A DETENTION FACILITY;

98 (II) PROVIDE NOTICE TO THE PUBLIC OF THE PROPOSED
99 ZONING CLASSIFICATION AT LEAST 120 DAYS BEFORE ENACTING THE
100 CLASSIFICATION; AND

101 (III) SOLICIT AND HEAR PUBLIC COMMENTS ON THE
102 PROPOSED CLASSIFICATION IN AT LEAST TWO SEPARATE MEETINGS OPEN TO
103 THE PUBLIC.

104 (C) A PRIVATE ENTITY MAY NOT OPERATE OR OCCUPY A BUILDING,
105 STRUCTURE, OR OTHER REAL PROPERTY AS A DETENTION FACILITY
106 WITHOUT A USE AND OCCUPANCY AUTHORIZATION OR CHANGE IN USE AND
107 OCCUPANCY APPROVAL CONSISTENT WITH THIS SECTION.

108 (D) FOR PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION, A BUILDING, STRUCTURE, OR
109 OTHER REAL PROPERTY SHALL BE CONSIDERED PROPOSED FOR USE AS A
110 DETENTION FACILITY, REGARDLESS OF HOW THE USE IS DESCRIBED
111 IN AN APPLICATION OR PERMIT REQUEST, IF:

112 (1) INDIVIDUALS ARE INTENDED TO BE HELD BY A PRIVATE ENTITY
113 FOR ANY STAGE OF PROCESSING FOR A FEDERAL IMMIGRATION VIOLATION;

114 (2) INDIVIDUALS ARE INTENDED TO BE HELD IN INVOLUNTARY
115 CUSTODY AND WILL NOT BE FREE TO LEAVE; OR

116 (3) THE DESIGN, CONSTRUCTION, OR IMPROVEMENTS INCLUDE
117 FEATURES CONSISTENT WITH SECURE OR INVOLUNTARY CONFINEMENT,
118 INCLUDING:

119 (I) PERIMETER SECURITY MEASURES DESIGNED TO PREVENT
120 OCCUPANTS FROM LEAVING;

121 (II) CONTROLLED ACCESS POINTS; OR

122 (III) LOCKED HOLDING AREAS.

123 (E) (1) THE ATTORNEY GENERAL MAY BRING A CIVIL ACTION TO
124 ENFORCE THIS SECTION.

125 (2) THE COURT MAY ISSUE TEMPORARY, PRELIMINARY, OR
126 PERMANENT INJUNCTIVE RELIEF TO PREVENT AN ACT THAT WOULD CONSTITUTE A
127 VIOLATION OF THIS SECTION.

128 (F) A PRIVATE ENTITY THAT OPERATES OR ATTEMPTS TO OPERATE A
129 DETENTION FACILITY IN VIOLATION OF THIS SECTION IS SUBJECT TO
130 A CIVIL PENALTY NOT EXCEEDING $10,000 FOR EACH DAY THE VIOLATION
131 CONTINUES.

132 (G) THE REMEDIES PROVIDED UNDER THIS SECTION ARE IN ADDITION TO
133 ANY OTHER REMEDY AVAILABLE UNDER STATE OR LOCAL LAW.

134 SECTION 3. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That:

135 (a) Section 2 of this Act is contingent on any provision of § 1–103 of the
136 Correctional Services Article, as enacted by Section 1 of this Act, being rendered
137 unenforceable by a State or federal court as to any party in any litigation challenging
138 the validity of § 1–103 of the Correctional Services Article.

139 (b) As soon as practicable after the contingency described in subsection (a) of
140 this section occurring, but no later than 5 days after, the Office of the Attorney General
141 shall promptly notify the Department of Legislative Services.

142 (c) If notice of the contingency described in subsection (a) of this section is
143 received by the Department of Legislative Services on or before December 31, 2029,
144 Section 2 of this Act shall take effect on the date the notice is received by the Department
145 of Legislative Services in accordance with subsection (b) of this section.

146 (d) If notice of the receipt of the contingency described in subsection (a) of this
147 section is not received by the Department of Legislative Services on or before December
148 31, 2029, Section 2 of this Act, with no further action required by the General Assembly,
149 shall be null and void.
