12 AUTHORIZATION FOR A BUILDING, A STRUCTURE, OR OTHER REAL PROPERTY TO

14 (3) BEFORE ESTABLISHING A ZONING CLASSIFICATION AUTHORIZING
15 PRIVATE DETENTION FACILITIES, THE STATE OR A UNIT OF LOCAL GOVERNMENT
16 SHALL:

17 (I) CONSIDER:

18 1. CONSISTENCY WITH ANY COMPREHENSIVE PLAN FOR
19 THE SURROUNDING AREA;

20 2. COMPATIBILITY WITH SURROUNDING LAND USES; AND

21 3. THE ABILITY OF LOCAL INFRASTRUCTURE TO
22 ADEQUATELY SUPPORT A DETENTION FACILITY;

23 (II) PROVIDE NOTICE TO THE PUBLIC OF THE PROPOSED ZONING
24 CLASSIFICATION AT LEAST 120 DAYS BEFORE ENACTING THE CLASSIFICATION; AND

25 (III) SOLICIT AND HEAR PUBLIC COMMENTS ON THE PROPOSED
26 CLASSIFICATION IN AT LEAST TWO SEPARATE MEETINGS OPEN TO THE PUBLIC.

27 (C) A PRIVATE ENTITY MAY NOT OPERATE OR OCCUPY A BUILDING, A

19 (a) Section 2 of this Act is contingent on any provision of § 1–103 of the

24 section occurring, but no later than 5 days after, the Office of the Attorney General shall
25 promptly notify the Department of Legislative Services.

26 (c) If notice of the contingency described in subsection (a) of this section is received
27 by the Department of Legislative Services on or before December 31, 2029, Section 2 of this
28 Act shall take effect on the date the notice is received by the Department of Legislative
29 Services in accordance with subsection (b) of this section.
