Caroline County Board of Education - Member Compensation - Increase 
 
 
This bill increases the annual compensation for members of the Caroline County Board of 
Education by $2,000. Under the bill, compensation for the board president increases to 
$6,000 annually and compensation for other voting members increases to $5,500 annually. 
In accordance with Article III of the Maryland Constitution, increases in compensation do 
not apply to board members currently serving in a term of office and will only take effect 
at the start of the term of an individual elected after the effective date of the bill. The bill 
takes effect July 1, 2026. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  None.    
  
Local Effect:  Caroline County school expenditures increase by $2,300 in FY 2027, rising 
to $10,000 beginning in FY 2030, reflecting compensation increases taking effect at the 
start of each member’s new term of office.  
  
Small Business Effect:  None.     
  
 
Analysis 
 
Current Law:  The Caroline County Board of Education is a hybrid appointed and elected 
school board comprising of three elected members, two appointed members, and 
two nonvoting student members. Elected members must be residents of and elected only 
by the voters of the three board of education districts in the county. The two appointed 
members must be appointed by the Governor with the advice and consent of the Senate, 
from the county at-large.

Local Expenditures:  Generally, voting members of the Caroline County Board of 
Education serve a term of four years beginning on the first Monday in December. Of the 
three elected members of the board, two are elected in presidential election years and one is 
elected in gubernatorial election years. Of the two appointed members, one is appointed in 
presidential election years and one is appointed in gubernatorial election years. Therefore, 
the next term of office for which the bill’s salary increases will apply begins  
December 2026, slightly less than halfway through fiscal 2027. By fiscal 2030, all 
five voting members of the board are subject to the increased salaries specified in the bill 
for the full fiscal year as shown in Exhibit 1.
