Maryland Transit Administration - Fifth Bus Division Facility - Construction 
 
 
This bill requires the Maryland Transit Administration (MTA) to complete construction of 
a fifth bus division facility by June 30, 2032. Beginning with the Consolidated 
Transportation Program (CTP) for fiscal 2027 through 2033, the Maryland Department of 
Transportation (MDOT) and MTA must identify the funding necessary to ensure that the 
fifth bus division facility is in operation by June 30, 2032. The bill takes effect  
July 1, 2026.  
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  Because MDOT’s capital program is generally fully subscribed each year, 
MDOT must redirect hundreds of millions of dollars from other projects in its capital 
program from FY 2027 through 2032 to ensure the fifth bus division facility is in operation 
by June 30, 2032, as discussed below. Transportation Trust Fund (TTF) expenditures 
increase significantly, likely beginning in FY 2032, for operating costs associated with the 
new facility, as discussed below. Revenues are not directly affected.  
 
Local Effect:  The bill does not directly affect local government operations or finances. 
 
Small Business Effect:  Minimal.

The CTP for fiscal 2026 through 2031 includes $14.6 million, primarily in  
fiscal 2028 and 2029, for engineering and right-of-way acquisition costs associated with 
the design and construction of a fifth bus division to serve MTA core bus transit.  
 
State Expenditures:  MDOT advises that the fifth bus division facility project is being 
considered as part of its Development and Evaluation Program, and, accordingly, the CTP 
for fiscal 2026 through 2031 includes funding only for the initial steps of the project; it 
does not include funding for construction of the facility. Therefore, to meet the bill’s 
requirement that the bus division be operational by June 30, 2032, MDOT must redirect 
significant funding from other projects included in the CTP. A preliminary analysis by 
MDOT estimates that, from fiscal 2027 through 2032, approximately $650.0 million must 
be redirected for construction costs for the project, and approximately $365.0 million must 
be redirected to procure additional buses for the facility.  
 
In addition, TTF expenditures associated with operating costs for the new facility are 
anticipated to increase significantly, likely beginning in fiscal 2032, as necessary staff are 
hired and trained to ensure the facility is operational by June 30, 2032, the final day of 
fiscal 2032. Beginning in fiscal 2033, once the facility is fully operational, MDOT 
anticipates ongoing operating costs of approximately $130.0 million annually.  
 
 
Additional Information 
 
Recent Prior Introductions:  Similar legislation has not been introduced within the last 
three years.
