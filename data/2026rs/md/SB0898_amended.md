CHAPTER ______

1 AN ACT concerning

2 Maryland Medical Assistance Program – Behavior Technicians –
3 Reimbursement

4 FOR the purpose of requiring the Maryland Department of Health to provide
5 reimbursement for applied behavior analysis services provided by behavior
6 technicians under the Maryland Medical Assistance Program; prohibiting,
7 subject to a certain exception, the Department from requiring as a condition of
8 reimbursement that an individual receive a certain certification to provide applied
9 behavior analysis services as a behavior technician to Program recipients;
10 and generally relating to the Maryland Medical Assistance Program and applied
11 behavior analysis services.

12 BY adding to
13 Article – Health – General
14 Section 15–114.2
15 Annotated Code of Maryland
16 (2023 Replacement Volume and 2025 Supplement)

17 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND,
18 That the Laws of Maryland read as follows:

19 Article – Health – General

20 15–114.2.

1 (A) (1) IN THIS SECTION THE FOLLOWING WORDS HAVE THE MEANINGS
2 INDICATED.

3 (2) (I) “APPLIED BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS” MEANS THE DESIGN,
4 IMPLEMENTATION, AND EVALUATION OF ENVIRONMENTAL MODIFICATIONS USING
5 BEHAVIORAL STIMULI AND CONSEQUENCES TO PRODUCE SOCIALLY SIGNIFICANT
6 IMPROVEMENTS IN HUMAN BEHAVIOR.

7 (II) “APPLIED BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS” INCLUDES THE DIRECT
8 OBSERVATIONS, MEASUREMENT, AND FUNCTIONAL ANALYSIS OF THE RELATIONS
9 BETWEEN ENVIRONMENT AND BEHAVIOR.

10 (3) “AUTISM SPECTRUM DISORDER” MEANS A GROUP OF
11 DEVELOPMENTAL DISORDERS:

12 (I) CHARACTERIZED BY PERSISTENT DEFICITS IN SOCIAL
13 COMMUNICATION AND SOCIAL INTERACTION ACROSS MULTIPLE CONTEXTS;

14 (II) CHARACTERIZED BY RESTRICTED, REPETITIVE PATTERNS
15 OF BEHAVIOR, INTERESTS, OR ACTIVITIES; AND

16 (III) WHOSE SYMPTOMS CAUSE CLINICALLY SIGNIFICANT
17 IMPAIRMENT IN SOCIAL, OCCUPATIONAL, OR OTHER IMPORTANT AREAS OF
18 CURRENT FUNCTIONING.

19 (4) “BEHAVIOR TECHNICIAN” MEANS AN INDIVIDUAL WHO:

20 (I) DELIVERS APPLIED BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS SERVICES
21 THAT ARE PROVIDED TO INDIVIDUALS WITH AUTISM SPECTRUM DISORDER
22 UNDER THE SUPERVISION OF:

23 1. A LICENSED PSYCHOLOGIST;

24 2. A BOARD CERTIFIED BEHAVIOR ANALYST–
25 DOCTORAL; OR

26 3. A BOARD CERTIFIED BEHAVIOR ANALYST; AND

27 (II) MEETS THE REQUIREMENTS FOR PROVIDING BEHAVIOR
28 ANALYSIS SERVICES UNDER THE PROGRAM ESTABLISHED BY THE DEPARTMENT
29 IN REGULATION.

30 (5) “BOARD CERTIFIED BEHAVIOR ANALYST” MEANS A LICENSED
31 PROFESSIONAL WHO:

32 (I) RENDERS AND PROVIDES DIRECTION FOR APPLIED
33 BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS SERVICES THAT ARE PROVIDED TO INDIVIDUALS WITH AUTISM
34 SPECTRUM DISORDER; AND

35 (II) HAS OBTAINED A BOARD CERTIFIED BEHAVIOR ANALYST
36 CERTIFICATION.

37 (6) “BOARD CERTIFIED BEHAVIOR ANALYST–DOCTORAL” MEANS A
38 LICENSED PROFESSIONAL WHO:

39 (I) RENDERS AND PROVIDES DIRECTION FOR APPLIED
40 BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS TREATMENTS THAT ARE PROVIDED TO INDIVIDUALS WITH
41 AUTISM SPECTRUM DISORDER; AND

1 (II) HAS OBTAINED A BOARD CERTIFIED BEHAVIOR
2 ANALYST–DOCTORAL CERTIFICATION.

3 (B) (1) THE DEPARTMENT SHALL PROVIDE REIMBURSEMENT FOR
4 APPLIED BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS SERVICES PROVIDED BY BEHAVIOR
5 TECHNICIANS TO PROGRAM RECIPIENTS WITH AUTISM SPECTRUM DISORDER.

6 (2) EXCEPT AS PROVIDED UNDER PARAGRAPH (3) OF THIS
7 SUBSECTION, THE DEPARTMENT MAY NOT REQUIRE AS A CONDITION OF
8 REIMBURSEMENT UNDER THE PROGRAM THAT AN INDIVIDUAL RECEIVE A
9 CERTIFICATION FROM A CREDENTIALING ENTITY RECOGNIZED BY THE
10 DEPARTMENT TO PROVIDE APPLIED BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS SERVICES AS A
11 BEHAVIOR TECHNICIAN.

12 (3) THE DEPARTMENT MAY REQUIRE AS A CONDITION OF
13 REIMBURSEMENT UNDER THE PROGRAM THAT AN INDIVIDUAL OBTAIN A CURRENT
14 CERTIFICATION ISSUED BY A CREDENTIALING ENTITY RECOGNIZED BY THE
15 DEPARTMENT TO PROVIDE APPLIED BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS
16 SERVICES AS A BEHAVIOR TECHNICIAN WITHIN 150 DAYS AFTER THE
17 INDIVIDUAL BEGINS PROVIDING APPLIED BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS SERVICES TO
18 PROGRAM RECIPIENTS AS A BEHAVIOR TECHNICIAN AND MAINTAIN
19 THE CERTIFICATION.

20 (C) THE DEPARTMENT SHALL ADOPT REGULATIONS NECESSARY TO CARRY
21 OUT THIS SECTION.

22 SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That this Act shall be construed to
23 apply only prospectively and may not be applied or interpreted to have any effect on or
24 application to any individual who provides applied behavior analysis services before the
25 effective date of this Act.

26 SECTION 3. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That:

27 (a) If, on or before June 30, 2027, the U.S. Department of Health and Human
28 Services advises or notifies the Maryland Department of Health by written policy, audit
29 finding, or other guidance that Section 1 of this Act is inconsistent with § 1902(a)(27) of
30 the federal Social Security Act, this Act, with no further action required by the General
31 Assembly, shall be abrogated and of no further force and effect.

32 (b) The Maryland Department of Health shall notify the Department of
33 Legislative Services within 5 days after receiving the advisement or notice from the U.S.
34 Department of Health and Human Services described under subsection (a) of this
35 section.

36 SECTION 4. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That this Act shall take effect
37 October 1, 2026.
