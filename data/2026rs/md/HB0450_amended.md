A BILL ENTITLED

1 AN ACT concerning

2 Criminal Procedure – Protection of Identity of Victim of Sexual Assault or
3 Stalking

4 FOR the purpose of prohibiting a court or a party in a criminal or juvenile delinquency case
5 from disclosing or allowing inspection of a certain court filing or record to a nonparty
6 unless the court or the party redacts certain identifying information relating to a
7 certain victim of sexual assault or victim of stalking that appears in the filing or
8 record, subject to a certain exception; requiring a court to redact certain identifying
9 information relating to a certain victim of sexual assault or victim of stalking from
10 an existing filing or record on certain request; providing for disclosure or inspection
11 of a certain record or filing by a certain health occupations board; and generally
12 relating to protection of victims.

1 (2) “Identifying information” means the name of, and any other
2 information that could reasonably be expected to identify, a minor victim, A VICTIM OF
3 SEXUAL ASSAULT, OR A VICTIM OF STALKING.

4 (3) “Minor victim” means [a] AN ALLEGED victim of a crime or
5 delinquent act who was a minor at the time that the crime or delinquent act occurred.

6 (4) “VICTIM OF SEXUAL ASSAULT” MEANS AN ALLEGED VICTIM
7 OF SEXUALLY ASSAULTIVE BEHAVIOR AS DEFINED IN § 10–923 OF THE COURTS
8 ARTICLE OR A CRIME UNDER TITLE 3, SUBTITLE 11 OF THE CRIMINAL LAW
9 ARTICLE.

10 (5) “VICTIM OF STALKING” MEANS AN ALLEGED VICTIM OF
11 STALKING AS DEFINED IN § 3–802 OF THE CRIMINAL LAW ARTICLE.

12 (B) ON MOTION OF THE STATE OR ON REQUEST OF A VICTIM OR WITNESS,
13 DURING A CRIMINAL TRIAL OR A JUVENILE DELINQUENCY ADJUDICATORY
14 HEARING, A COURT MAY PROHIBIT THE RELEASE OF THE ADDRESS OR
15 TELEPHONE NUMBER OF THE VICTIM OR WITNESS UNLESS THE COURT
16 DETERMINES THAT GOOD CAUSE IS SHOWN FOR THE RELEASE OF THE
17 INFORMATION.

18 (C) Notwithstanding any other law, on notice that an electronic or paper
19 filing includes identifying information of a minor victim, unless the court finds by clear and
20 convincing evidence that there is good cause to order otherwise, the court or a party in a
21 criminal or juvenile delinquency case may not disclose or allow inspection of an electronic
22 or paper court filing, including a charging document, to a nonparty to the criminal or
23 juvenile delinquency case unless the court or the party disclosing or allowing inspection of
24 the filing redacts all identifying information that appears in the filing.

25 (D) (1) NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER LAW, ON NOTICE THAT A CASE
26 FILING OR RECORD INCLUDES IDENTIFYING INFORMATION OF A VICTIM OF SEXUAL
27 ASSAULT OR A VICTIM OF STALKING, UNLESS THE COURT FINDS CLEAR AND
28 CONVINCING EVIDENCE THAT THERE IS GOOD CAUSE TO ORDER OTHERWISE, THE
29 COURT OR A PARTY IN A CRIMINAL OR JUVENILE DELINQUENCY CASE MAY NOT
30 DISCLOSE OR ALLOW INSPECTION OF AN ELECTRONIC OR PAPER COURT FILING OR
31 RECORD, INCLUDING A CHARGING DOCUMENT, TO A NONPARTY TO THE CRIMINAL
32 OR JUVENILE DELINQUENCY CASE, EXCEPT TO THE VICTIM OR THE VICTIM’S
33 REPRESENTATIVE, UNLESS THE COURT OR THE PARTY DISCLOSING OR ALLOWING
34 INSPECTION OF THE FILING OR RECORD REDACTS ALL IDENTIFYING INFORMATION
35 THAT APPEARS IN THE FILING OR RECORD.

36 (2) ON WRITTEN REQUEST OF A VICTIM OF SEXUAL ASSAULT OR A
37 VICTIM OF STALKING, THE COURT SHALL REDACT IDENTIFYING INFORMATION
38 RELATING TO THE VICTIM FROM AN EXISTING FILING OR RECORD.
