House Joint Resolution 10 
(Delegate J. Long) 
Rules and Executive Nominations 
 
 
Condemning Violence and Violations of Law - Russian Federation in Ukraine 
 
 
This joint resolution declares (1) the abduction and forcible transfer of children and 
facilitation of illegal adoptions is contrary to Russia’s obligations under the Genocide 
Convention and (2) the Russian Federation is attempting to wipe out a generation of 
Ukrainian children, thereby impeding Ukraine’s ability to nurture the next generation of 
Ukrainian citizens and leaders and to rebuild their country after Russia’s unprovoked war. 
The General Assembly officially condemns these and other actions in the strongest terms. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  The joint resolution does not directly affect State finances. 
  
Local Effect:  None. 
  
Small Business Effect:  None. 
  
 
Analysis 
 
Bill Summary:  The General Assembly notes with concern that the invasion of Ukraine 
by the Russian Federation has significantly increased the risks of children being exposed 
to various traumas including human trafficking, exploitation, child labor, gender-based 
violence, hunger, injury, deprivation of education, deprivation of shelter, and death. 
 
The General Assembly holds the government of the Russian Federation responsible for the 
wrongful and illegal abduction, forced transfer, and subsequent illegal adoption and 
indoctrination of children from Ukraine.

Current Law:  In 1948 the United Nations adopted the Convention on the Prevention and 
Punishment of the Crime of Genocide. The Genocide Convention was entered into force 
in 1951.  
 
The General Assembly adopted Joint Resolutions 6 and 7 of 2022, which declared that the 
State stands in solidarity with the government and people of Ukraine as they resist the 
unprovoked invasion of their territory by the Russian Federation. The joint resolutions 
encouraged all Marylanders to assist in providing humanitarian assistance to the people of 
Ukraine affected by the conflict.
