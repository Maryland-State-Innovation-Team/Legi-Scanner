CHAPTER ______

1 AN ACT concerning

2 Business Regulation – Rounding Cash Transactions – Authorization

3 FOR the purpose of authorizing a merchant to round the portion of the price of a
4 transaction for which a customer pays using cash in a certain manner or round the
5 amount of change due to a customer in a certain manner; PROVIDING THAT A CERTAIN
6 AMOUNT ADDED TO OR SUBTRACTED FROM THE PRICE IN A CASH TRANSACTION
7 UNDER THIS ACT IS NOT INCLUDED IN THE TAXABLE PRICE; providing that certain
8 provisions of this Act shall preempt existing regulations, rules, and ordinances that
9 conflict with certain provisions of this Act; altering the definition of “taxable price” for
10 the purposes of certain provisions of law governing the State sales and use tax to clarify
11 the exclusion from the tax of a certain amount collected due to rounding the price for
12 a good or service in accordance with certain provisions of this Act; and generally
13 relating to rounding cash transactions.

1 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND,
2 That the Laws of Maryland read as follows:

3 Article – Business Regulation

4 SUBTITLE 6. ROUNDING CASH TRANSACTIONS IN A TRADE OR BUSINESS.

5 1–601.

6 (A) IN THIS SUBTITLE THE FOLLOWING WORDS HAVE THE MEANINGS
7 INDICATED.

8 (B) (1) “CASH” MEANS UNITED STATES COINS AND CURRENCY.

9 (2) “CASH” DOES NOT INCLUDE:

10 (I) A PAPER INSTRUMENT OTHER THAN A FEDERAL RESERVE
11 NOTE; OR

12 (II) A GIFT CARD.

13 (C) “CUSTOMER” MEANS A PURCHASER OF A GOOD OR SERVICE FROM A
14 MERCHANT.

15 (D) “MERCHANT” MEANS A PERSON ENGAGED IN THE TRADE OR BUSINESS
16 OF THE SALE OF GOODS OR SERVICES.

17 (E) “PRICE” MEANS THE AMOUNT DUE AND PAYABLE BY A
18 CUSTOMER FOR A GOOD OR SERVICE AFTER A MERCHANT:

19 (1) SUBTRACTS ANY DISCOUNT OR DEDUCTION; AND

20 (2) APPLIES ANY APPLICABLE TAX OR FEE.

21 1–602.

22 THIS SUBTITLE:

23 (1) DOES NOT APPLY TO A TRANSACTION FOR A GOOD OR SERVICE
24 PAID WITH EXACT CHANGE; AND

25 (2) MAY NOT BE CONSTRUED TO AUTHORIZE A MERCHANT TO
26 ROUND A PRICE FOR A GOOD OR SERVICE IN A MANNER THAT ALTERS THE
27 CALCULATION OF ANY TAX OR FEE APPLICABLE TO THE TRANSACTION.

1 1–603.

2 (A) THIS SECTION APPLIES TO THE PORTION OF THE PRICE OF AN
3 IN–PERSON TRANSACTION OR A TELEPHONE, MAIL, OR INTERNET TRANSACTION
4 FOR WHICH A CUSTOMER PAYS USING CASH IF THE PRICE TOTALS AT LEAST 5 CENTS.

5 (B) NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER PROVISION OF LAW, A MERCHANT
6 DOING BUSINESS IN THE STATE MAY:

7 (1) ROUND THE PRICE FOR A GOOD OR SERVICE IN THE FOLLOWING
8 MANNER:

9 (I) IF THE PRICE ENDS IN 1 CENT, 2 CENTS, 6 CENTS, OR 7
10 CENTS, BY ROUNDING THE PRICE DOWN TO THE NEAREST CENT DIVISIBLE BY FIVE;
11 OR

12 (II) IF THE PRICE ENDS IN 3 CENTS, 4 CENTS, 8 CENTS, OR 9
13 CENTS, BY ROUNDING THE PRICE UP TO THE NEAREST CENT DIVISIBLE BY FIVE; OR

14 (2) ROUND THE AMOUNT OF CHANGE DUE TO A CUSTOMER UP OR
15 DOWN CONSISTENT WITH ITEM (1) OF THIS SUBSECTION.

16 1–604.

17 (A) THIS SECTION APPLIES TO THE PORTION OF THE PRICE OF AN
18 IN–PERSON TRANSACTION OR A TELEPHONE, MAIL, OR INTERNET TRANSACTION
19 FOR WHICH A CUSTOMER PAYS USING CASH IF THE PRICE TOTALS LESS THAN 5
20 CENTS.

21 (B) NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER PROVISION OF LAW, A MERCHANT
22 DOING BUSINESS IN THE STATE MAY ROUND THE PRICE FOR A GOOD OR SERVICE
23 BY ROUNDING THE PRICE UP TO 5 CENTS.

24 1–605.

25 THIS SUBTITLE SUPERSEDES ANY REGULATION, RULE, OR ORDINANCE THAT
26 CONFLICTS WITH THIS SUBTITLE.

27 1–606.

28 THE SECRETARY MAY ADOPT REGULATIONS TO CARRY OUT THE PROVISIONS
29 OF THIS SUBTITLE.
