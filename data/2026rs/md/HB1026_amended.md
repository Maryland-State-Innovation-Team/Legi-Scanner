CHAPTER ______

1 AN ACT concerning

2 Business Regulation – Rounding the portion of the price of a transaction for which a customer pays using cash – Authorization

3 authorizing a merchant to round the portion of the price of a certain
4 transaction for which a customer pays using cash in a certain manner or round the
5 amount of change due to a customer in a certain manner; providing that a certain 
6 amount added to or subtracted from the price in a cash transaction under this Act is 
7 not included in the taxable price; providing that certain
8 provisions of this Act shall preempt existing regulations, rules, and ordinances that
9 conflict with certain provisions of this Act; altering the definition of “taxable price”
10 for the purposes of certain provisions of law governing the State sales and use tax
11 to clarify the exclusion from the tax of a certain amount collected due to rounding
12 the price for a good or service in accordance with certain provisions of this Act;
13 and generally relating to rounding cash transactions.
