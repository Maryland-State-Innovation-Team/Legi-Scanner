CHAPTER ______

1 AN ACT concerning

2 Business Regulation – Rounding Cash Transactions – Authorization

3 FOR the purpose of authorizing a merchant to round the portion of the price of a certain
4 transaction for which a customer pays using cash in a certain manner or round the
5 amount of change due to a customer in a certain manner; providing that a certain
6 amount added to or subtracted from the price in a cash transaction under this Act is
7 not included in the taxable price; providing that certain provisions of this Act shall
8 preempt existing regulations, rules, and ordinances that conflict with certain
9 provisions of this Act; and generally relating to rounding cash transactions.

3 Article – Business Regulation

4 SUBTITLE 6. ROUNDING CASH TRANSACTIONS IN A TRADE OR BUSINESS.

5 1–601.

6 (A) IN THIS SUBTITLE THE FOLLOWING WORDS HAVE THE MEANINGS
7 INDICATED.

8 (B) (1) “CASH” MEANS UNITED STATES COINS AND CURRENCY.

9 (2) “CASH” DOES NOT INCLUDE:

10 (I) A PAPER INSTRUMENT OTHER THAN A FEDERAL RESERVE
11 NOTE; OR

12 (II) A GIFT CARD.

13 (C) “CUSTOMER” MEANS A PURCHASER OF A GOOD OR SERVICE FROM A
14 MERCHANT.

15 (D) “MERCHANT” MEANS A PERSON ENGAGED IN THE TRADE OR BUSINESS
16 OF THE SALE OF GOODS OR SERVICES.

17 (E) “PRICE” MEANS THE AMOUNT DUE AND PAYABLE BY A CUSTOMER FOR
18 A GOOD OR SERVICE AFTER A MERCHANT:

19 (1) SUBTRACTS ANY DISCOUNT OR DEDUCTION; AND

20 (2) APPLIES ANY APPLICABLE TAX OR FEE.

21 1–602.

22 THIS SUBTITLE: (1) DOES NOT APPLY TO A TRANSACTION FOR A GOOD OR
23 SERVICE PAID WITH EXACT CHANGE; AND (2) MAY NOT BE CONSTRUED TO
24 AUTHORIZE A MERCHANT TO ROUND A PRICE FOR A GOOD OR SERVICE IN A
25 MANNER THAT ALTERS THE CALCULATION OF ANY TAX OR FEE APPLICABLE TO
26 THE TRANSACTION.

1 1–603.

2 (A) THIS SECTION APPLIES TO THE PORTION OF THE PRICE OF AN
3 IN–PERSON TRANSACTION OR A TELEPHONE, MAIL, OR INTERNET TRANSACTION
4 FOR WHICH A CUSTOMER PAYS USING CASH IF THE PRICE TOTALS AT LEAST 5
5 CENTS.

6 (B) NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER PROVISION OF LAW, A MERCHANT
7 DOING BUSINESS IN THE STATE MAY:

8 (1) ROUND THE PRICE FOR A GOOD OR SERVICE IN THE FOLLOWING
9 MANNER:

10 (I) IF THE PRICE ENDS IN 1 CENT, 2 CENTS, 6 CENTS, OR 7
11 CENTS, BY ROUNDING THE PRICE DOWN TO THE NEAREST CENT DIVISIBLE BY FIVE;
12 OR

13 (II) IF THE PRICE ENDS IN 3 CENTS, 4 CENTS, 8 CENTS, OR 9
14 CENTS, BY ROUNDING THE PRICE UP TO THE NEAREST CENT DIVISIBLE BY FIVE; OR

15 (2) ROUND THE AMOUNT OF CHANGE DUE TO A CUSTOMER UP OR
16 DOWN CONSISTENT WITH ITEM (1) OF THIS SUBSECTION.

17 1–604.

18 (A) THIS SECTION APPLIES TO THE PORTION OF THE PRICE OF AN
19 IN–PERSON TRANSACTION OR A TELEPHONE, MAIL, OR INTERNET TRANSACTION
20 FOR WHICH A CUSTOMER PAYS USING CASH IF THE PRICE TOTALS LESS THAN 5
21 CENTS.

22 (B) NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER PROVISION OF LAW, A MERCHANT
23 DOING BUSINESS IN THE STATE MAY ROUND THE PRICE FOR A GOOD OR
24 SERVICE BY ROUNDING THE PRICE UP TO 5 CENTS.
