2 HOUSE BILL 1557

1 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND,
2 That the Laws of Maryland read as follows:

3 Article – State Finance and Procurement

4 4–417.

5 (A) IN THIS SECTION, “COMMITTEE” MEANS THE REAL ESTATE COMMITTEE.

6 (B) THERE IS A REAL ESTATE COMMITTEE IN THE DEPARTMENT.

7 (C) THE PURPOSE OF THE COMMITTEE IS TO ADVISE THE DEPARTMENT,
8 THE BOARD OF PUBLIC WORKS, AND THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY ON INDUSTRY
9 TRENDS, DEVELOPMENTS AND MOVEMENT IN THE COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE
10 SECTOR, AND BEST PRACTICES FOR REAL ESTATE ASSET MANAGEMENT IN THE
11 STATE.

12 (D) THE COMMITTEE CONSISTS OF THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS:

13 (1) THE SECRETARY, OR THE SECRETARY’S DESIGNEE;

14 (2) FOUR MEMBERS APPOINTED BY THE SECRETARY;

15 (3) ONE MEMBER APPOINTED BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE SENATE;
16 AND

17 (4) ONE MEMBER APPOINTED BY THE SPEAKER OF THE HOUSE.

18 (E) THE SECRETARY SHALL DESIGNATE THE CHAIR OF THE COMMITTEE.

19 (F) THE DEPARTMENT AND THE DEPARTMENT OF LEGISLATIVE SERVICES
20 SHALL PROVIDE STAFF FOR THE COMMITTEE.

21 (G) A MEMBER OF THE COMMITTEE:

22 (1) MAY NOT RECEIVE COMPENSATION AS A MEMBER OF THE
23 COMMITTEE; BUT

24 (2) IS ENTITLED TO REIMBURSEMENT FOR EXPENSES UNDER THE
25 STANDARD STATE TRAVEL REGULATIONS, AS PROVIDED IN THE STATE BUDGET.

26 (H) THE COMMITTEE:

27 (1) SHALL MEET AT LEAST THREE TIMES ANNUALLY; AND

28 (2) SHALL REVIEW, AT LEAST ONCE EACH YEAR, THE STATE’S
29 LONG–TERM REAL ESTATE PLANS AND GOALS.
