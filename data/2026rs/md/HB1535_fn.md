Natural Resources - Recreational Fishing - Mobile Reporting Application 
 
   
This bill requires the Department of Natural Resources (DNR) to develop a mobile 
application (“app”), for use by angler’s licensees and Chesapeake Bay and coastal sport 
fishing licensees, to submit any report required by the department under specified statutory 
provisions. DNR may contract with a third party to develop the mobile app. The bill takes 
effect July 1, 2026. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  General fund expenditures increase by at least $736,800 in FY 2027. Future 
years reflect annualization and inflation.

Current Law:   
 
Recreational Fishing Licenses and Reporting 
 
Chapter 520 of 2024 requires persons licensed to fish recreationally (under an angler’s 
license or a Chesapeake Bay and coastal sport fishing license) to submit any report DNR

requires in accordance with DNR regulations. In general, any person age 16 or older  
(1) must secure an angler’s license to fish in the nontidal waters of the State and (2) must 
obtain a Chesapeake Bay and coastal sport fishing (recreational tidal) license before fishing 
for finfish in the tidal waters of the State, including the Chesapeake Bay and its tributaries, 
the State waters of the Atlantic Ocean, and the Atlantic coastal bays. 
 
Under the provisions governing the Chesapeake Bay and coastal sport fishing license, DNR 
is authorized to provide by regulation for the issuance of an annual special Chesapeake 
Bay and coastal sport fishing license (which DNR calls a “consolidated Chesapeake Bay 
and coastal sport boat license”), which when permanently affixed to a boat (other than a 
boat hired to take persons fishing) authorizes any person on the boat to fish under the 
license, provided the person registers with DNR. 
 
Chesapeake Bay and Coastal Sport Fishing License Pilot Program 
 
Chapters 409 and 410 of 2022 established a Chesapeake Bay and Coastal Sport Fishing 
License Pilot Program in DNR. The purpose of the pilot program is to (1) collect contact 
information and demographic data of individuals fishing under a consolidated 
Chesapeake Bay and coastal sport boat license; (2) collect information regarding the 
number of trips taken and fish caught and released under a consolidated Chesapeake Bay 
and coastal sport boat license; and (3) improve compliance with the provision under the 
Chesapeake Bay and coastal sport fishing license statute that requires certain individuals 
authorized to fish without a license (including those fishing on a boat that has a 
consolidated Chesapeake Bay and coastal sport boat license affixed to it) to register with 
DNR.  
 
The department must track monthly costs associated with implementing the pilot program, 
and each December 1, for the duration of the program, report recommendations on 
expected funding needed to grow and establish the program as a permanent program, to 
the Governor and the General Assembly. The provisions of Chapters 409 and 410 that 
establish the pilot program terminate June 30, 2027. 
 
State Fiscal Effect:  General fund expenditures increase by at least $736,750 in 
fiscal 2027, at least $350,530 in fiscal 2028, and by similar ongoing minimum amounts in 
future years, for DNR to develop and implement a mobile app for use by angler’s and 
Chesapeake Bay and coastal sport fishing licensees to report recreational catch information 
to the department. This estimate accounts for the bill’s July 1, 2026 effective date. The 
costs are assumed to be covered by general funds, due to limited availability of 
special funds for fisheries management. The estimate includes a salary, fringe benefits, 
one-time start-up costs, and ongoing operating expenses. It accounts for:

 
development costs to modify DNR’s MD Outdoors licensing mobile app to be

This minimum estimate assumes that DNR configures and uses the MD Outdoors licensing 
mobile app for voluntary reporting by recreational licensees as a result of the bill, requiring 
the natural resources biologist to oversee the development, maintenance, and use of the app 
and the data collected through it. The estimate also assumes that configuration of the 
MD Outdoors mobile app is complete by January 1, 2027 (though presumably the 
configuration could take longer). To the extent a more significant, mandatory reporting 
program is established, additional costs are incurred for an additional staff position, 
outreach to licensees, additional app development/configuration and maintenance/storage, 
and data entry of paper reports submitted by licensees who do not have access to the app. 
 
For additional context regarding this estimate being a minimum estimate, DNR indicates 
that configuring the MD Outdoors app for reporting of recreational licensees’ catch will 
provide the department a certain level of data collection ability, but development of an 
independent, more comprehensive, and high-level catch reporting mobile app (providing 
more effective data for fishery management) results in costs upwards of $2.0 million for 
development (for reporting covering various tidal and nontidal recreational fisheries), and 
potentially upwards of $500,000 for annual maintenance and data storage.
