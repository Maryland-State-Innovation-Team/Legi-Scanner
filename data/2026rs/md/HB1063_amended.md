CHAPTER ______

1 AN ACT concerning

2 Natural Resources – Hunting – Management

3 For the purpose of providing that the Department of Natural Resources may authorize the
4 reduction of the deer population on certain property with deer damage using certain
5 methods; ALTERING THE TIME RESTRICTIONS FOR SUNDAY DEER HUNTING IN CERTAIN
6 COUNTIES DURING DEER FIREARMS SEASON; repealing certain time restrictions that
7 apply to the hunting of game birds and mammals on certain Sundays on private
8 property in Wicomico County; authorizing the Department to allow a person to hunt
9 deer on each Sunday of the deer bow hunting season in Baltimore City;
10 repealing certain provisions of law governing Sunday hunting on a
11 county–by–county basis and authorizing the Department to adopt regulations
12 allowing Sunday hunting during game bird and game mammal seasons, subject to a
13 certain standard; REQUIRING THE DEPARTMENT TO ESTABLISH CERTAIN PROCEDURES FOR A COUNTY TO REQUEST CHANGES TO SUNDAY HUNTING REGULATIONS; and generally relating to hunting management.

5 (A) The Department may reduce the wildlife population in any county, election
6 district, or other identifiable area of the State after a thorough investigation reveals that
7 protected wildlife is seriously injurious to agricultural or other interests in the affected
8 area. The method of reducing the wildlife population is at the Department’s discretion,
9 except that trapping is preferred whenever feasible. The Department shall dispose of
10 wildlife taken pursuant to the provisions of this section as the Department deems
11 advisable.

12 (B) (1) IN THIS SUBSECTION, “DEER DAMAGE” INCLUDES:

13 (I) WIDESPREAD DAMAGE CAUSED BY DEER, INCLUDING THE
14 ELIMINATION OF FOREST UNDERSTORY, TREE REGENERATION, AND NATIVE
15 PLANTS; AND

16 (II) THE DESTRUCTION OF AGRICULTURAL CROPS CAUSED BY DEER.

29 (1) a summary of any regulations adopted under § 10–410(a)(1) of the Natural Resources Article, as enacted by Section 2 of this Act, during the reporting period;
