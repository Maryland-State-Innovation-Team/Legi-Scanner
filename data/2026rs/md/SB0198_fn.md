Prince George's Community College - Aerospace and Aviation Systems 
Technology Programs - Feasibility Study 
 
 
This bill requires Prince George’s Community College (PGCC) to conduct a 
comprehensive feasibility study and program design assessment to establish an aerospace 
systems technology workforce training program and an aviation systems technology 
workforce training program, as specified. For fiscal 2028, the Governor must include in 
the annual budget bill an appropriation of $100,000 to PGCC for the study and design 
assessment. By December 1, 2027, PGCC must report to the Senate Education, Energy and 
the Environment Committee and the House Ways and Means Committee with specified 
findings, recommendations, and plans. The bill takes effect July 1, 2026, and terminates 
June 30, 2028. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  General fund expenditures increase by $250,000 in FY 2027 and $100,000 
in FY 2028 in accordance with funding included in the FY 2027 budget as passed by the 
General Assembly and the bill’s mandate. State agencies, including the Maryland Higher 
Education Commission, the Maryland Department of Labor, and the Maryland Department 
of Commerce can likely consult on the study with existing resources. Revenues are not 
affected. This bill establishes a mandated appropriation for FY 2028.

Local Effect:  PGCC revenues and expenditures correspondingly increase by $250,000 in 
FY 2027 and $100,000 in FY 2028 from State appropriations and to conduct the feasibility 
study. 
 
Small Business Effect:  Minimal. 
 
 
Analysis 
 
Bill Summary:  The study must include an examination of specified aspects of program 
design and implementation. PGCC must consult with specified stakeholders in conducting 
the study and the study must include structured industry engagement, including employer 
roundtables, workforce charrettes, and demand-validation sessions to ensure alignment 
with current and projected labor markets. 
 
Current Law:  PGCC currently does not offer a program of study in aerospace technology, 
though it does offer degrees in related fields, like its Associate of Science Degree in 
Engineering. 
 
Local Effect:  The fiscal 2027 budget as passed by the General Assembly includes 
$250,000 to study the feasibility of establishing an Aerospace Aviation Program at PGCC. 
PGCC can likely cover any expenses associated with conducting the feasibility study and 
program design assessment using revenues from the budgeted $250,000 in fiscal 2027 
funding and mandated $100,000 appropriation in fiscal 2028. 
 
 
Additional Information 
 
Recent Prior Introductions:  Similar legislation has not been introduced within the last 
three years.
