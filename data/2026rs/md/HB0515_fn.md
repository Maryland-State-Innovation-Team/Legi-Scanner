Public Health - Local Drug Overdose Fatality Review Teams - Membership 
 
 
This bill alters the membership of a local drug overdose fatality review team, such that the 
representative of a hospital must serve as vice president, chief medical officer, or assistant 
medical officer at the hospital. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  None. The bill only affects local government operations. 
  
Local Effect:  The Maryland Association of County Health Officers advises that the bill 
has no material impact on local health department revenues or expenditures. 
 
Small Business Effect:  None. 
 
 
Analysis 
 
Current Law:  Chapter 650 of 2014 authorized the establishment of a multiagency and 
multidisciplinary local drug overdose fatality review team in each county. Local teams 
must meet at least quarterly to review the status of drug overdose death cases, recommend 
actions to improve coordination of services and investigations, and recommend actions 
within the member agencies to prevent overdose deaths. The membership of each local 
team must include individuals from law enforcement, health care, and social services, 
among other fields. 
 
The purpose of the local teams is to prevent drug overdose deaths by promoting 
cooperation and coordination among agencies involved in investigations of drug overdose 
deaths; developing an understanding of the causes and incidence of drug overdose deaths

Each team must consult with MDH to establish and implement a protocol for the local 
team; set a goal of investigating drug overdose deaths in accordance with national 
standards; and collect, maintain, and report data regarding overdose fatality deaths as 
required by MDH. Health care providers and State and local government agencies must 
provide a local team with access to relevant information and records.
