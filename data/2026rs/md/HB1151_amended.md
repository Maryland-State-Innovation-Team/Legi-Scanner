CHAPTER ______

1 AN ACT concerning

2 Maryland Department of Health – Study on Maryland Medical Assistance
3 Program Reimbursement of Hospice Room and Board Services

4 FOR the purpose of requiring the Maryland Health Care Commission and the
5 Maryland Medical Assistance Program jointly to study the impact of requiring the
6 Maryland Medical Assistance Program to provide reimbursement for room and board
7 services provided by a hospice house; and generally relating to a study of
8 reimbursement for hospice room and board services under the Maryland Medical
9 Assistance Program.

10 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND,
11 That:

1 (a) The Maryland Health Care Commission and the Maryland Medical
2 Assistance Program jointly shall study the impact of requiring the Maryland Medical
3 Assistance Program to provide reimbursement for room and board services provided by a
4 hospice house, as defined in COMAR 10.07.22.02.

5 (b) The study required under subsection (a) of this section shall:

6 (1) analyze overall Maryland Medical Assistance Program savings per
7 enrollee death associated with hospice care utilization during the last 6 months of life
8 compared with costs associated with other services provided to Program enrollees during the
9 last 6 months of life, including the impact on:

10 (i) avoided inpatient hospital stays;

11 (ii) emergency department utilization; and

12 (iii) intensive treatments near the end of life;

13 (2) analyze the projected fiscal impact of the provision of reimbursement
14 for hospice house room and board services, including any potential offsets or net savings;

15 (3) examine how the provision of reimbursement for room and board
16 services would:

17 (i) affect hospital readmission rates;

18 (ii) improve hospice length of stay;

19 (iii) improve early access to hospice services;

20 (iv) result in a reduction in deaths in hospitals; and

21 (v) result in a reduction in emergency department utilization; and

22 (4) include a comparison of other states that provide reimbursement for
23 hospice house room and board service, including a comparison of:

24 (i) payment models;

25 (ii) utilization outcomes; and

26 (iii) cost impacts.

1 (c) On or before December 1, 2026, the Maryland Department of
2 Health shall report its findings to the Senate Finance Committee and the House Health
3 Committee, in accordance with § 2–1257 of the State Government Article.

4 SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That this Act shall take effect July
5 1, 2026.
