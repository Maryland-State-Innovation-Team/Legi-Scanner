1 AN ACT concerning

2 Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program – Heat and Eat Program –
3 Study

4 FOR the purpose of requiring the Department of Human Services to study the potential number of
5 households excluded from automatic standard utility allowance eligibility for the
6 Heat and Eat Program within the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program due
7 to enactment of a certain federal law; and generally relating to a study on eligibility
8 for the Heat and Eat Program within the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance
9 Program.

1 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND,
2 That:

3 (a) The Department of Human Services shall study and make recommendations
4 on the potential number of households excluded from automatic standard utility allowance
5 eligibility for the Heat and Eat Program within the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance
6 Program due to enactment of the federal One Big Beautiful Bill Act of 2025.

7 (b) In conducting the study described in subsection (a) of this section, the
8 Department shall:

9 (1) compile information on the number of households that qualified for
10 automatic standard utility allowance eligibility for the Heat and Eat Program in each
