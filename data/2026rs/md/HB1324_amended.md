HOUSE BILL 1324 2

1 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND,
2 That the Laws of Maryland read as follows:

3 Article – Natural Resources

4 10–410.

5 (g) (1) Except as provided in paragraphs (2) and (3) of this subsection, a
6 person, other than the owner or occupant, while hunting for any wild bird or mammal may
7 not shoot or discharge any firearm or other deadly weapon within 150 yards, known as the
8 “safety zone”, of a dwelling house, residence, church, or any other building or camp occupied by
9 human beings, or shoot at any wild bird or mammal while it is within this area, without
10 the specific advance permission of the owner or occupant.

11 (3) (i) For archery hunters in Allegany County, BALTIMORE CITY, EXCEPT IN LEGISLATIVE DISTRICT 43A,
12 Calvert County, Carroll County, Cecil County, Frederick County, Garrett County, Harford
13 County, Montgomery County, St. Mary’s County, Washington County, Worcester County,
14 or Wicomico County, the safety zone described in paragraph (1) of this subsection extends
15 for 50 yards from a dwelling house, residence, church, or any other building or camp
16 occupied by human beings.

17 SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That this Act shall take effect July
18 1, 2026.
