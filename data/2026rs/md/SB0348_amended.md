1 (a) If a newborn is delivered in a hospital following a high–risk pregnancy, the
2 hospital shall:

3 (1) Complete a postpartum infant and maternal referral form and submit
4 the form to the local health department for the county in which the birthing parent resides;

5 (2) Provide to the birthing parent resources and information specific to the
6 circumstances of the birthing parent, including information regarding the risks, signs,
7 preventive measures, and treatment needs for postpartum complications, including
8 cardiovascular conditions, chronic disease, substance misuse, and mental health
9 conditions; and

10 (3) Call the birthing parent at least 24, but not later than [48]
11 72, hours after discharging the parent to evaluate the parent’s status and, as necessary,
12 provide information about postpartum complications.

13 (B) (1) IF A HOSPITAL HAS NOT MADE CONTACT WITH A BIRTHING
14 PARENT AFTER FIRST MAKING A CALL UNDER SUBSECTION (A)(3) OF THIS
15 SECTION, THE HOSPITAL SHALL SEEK TO MAKE DIRECT CONTACT WITH THE
16 BIRTHING PARENT BY:

17 (I) E–MAIL WITH RETURN RECEIPT REQUESTED;

18 (II) TEXT MESSAGE TO A DIRECT NUMBER; OR

19 (III) ANOTHER MEANS OF DIRECT COMMUNICATION.

20 (2) A HOSPITAL MAY NOT SATISFY THE REQUIREMENT OF
21 PARAGRAPH (1) OF THIS SUBSECTION BY SENDING A MESSAGE THROUGH A
22 PATIENT PORTAL.

23 (C) On or before October 1 each year, each local health department shall submit
24 to the Department a report that includes the number and type of referrals made based on
25 the referral forms submitted to the local health department in accordance with subsection
26 (a)(1) of this section.

27 19–3B–03.1.

28 (a) If a newborn is delivered in a freestanding birthing center following a
29 high–risk pregnancy, the freestanding birthing center shall:

30 (1) Complete a postpartum infant and maternal referral form and submit
31 the form to the local health department for the county in which the birthing parent resides;

32 (2) Provide to the birthing parent resources and information specific to the
33 circumstances of the birthing parent, including information regarding the risks, signs,
34 preventive measures, and treatment needs for postpartum complications, including
35 cardiovascular conditions, chronic disease, substance misuse, and mental health
36 conditions; and

37 (3) Call the birthing parent at least 24, but not later than [48]
38 72, hours after discharging the parent to evaluate the parent’s status and, as necessary,
39 provide information about postpartum complications.

40 (B) (1) IF A FREESTANDING BIRTHING CENTER HAS NOT MADE
41 CONTACT WITH A BIRTHING PARENT AFTER FIRST MAKING A CALL UNDER
42 SUBSECTION (A)(3) OF THIS SECTION, THE FREESTANDING BIRTHING CENTER
43 SHALL SEEK TO MAKE DIRECT CONTACT WITH THE BIRTHING PARENT BY:

44 (I) E–MAIL WITH RETURN RECEIPT REQUESTED;

45 (II) TEXT MESSAGE TO A DIRECT NUMBER; OR

46 (III) ANOTHER MEANS OF DIRECT COMMUNICATION.

47 (2) A FREESTANDING BIRTHING CENTER MAY NOT SATISFY THE
48 REQUIREMENT OF PARAGRAPH (1) OF THIS SUBSECTION BY SENDING A MESSAGE
49 THROUGH A PATIENT PORTAL.

50 (C) On or before October 1 each year, each local health department shall submit
51 to the Department a report that includes the number and type of referrals made based on
52 the referral forms submitted to the local health department in accordance with subsection
53 (a)(1) of this section.

54 SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That this Act shall take effect
