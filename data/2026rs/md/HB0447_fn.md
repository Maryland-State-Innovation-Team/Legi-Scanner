Natural Resources - Right to Hunt, Fish, and Harvest Wildlife 
 
 
This bill proposes a constitutional amendment that establishes under the Maryland 
Declaration of Rights a specified right of every person to hunt, fish, and harvest wildlife, 
including the right to use traditional methods to hunt, fish, and harvest wildlife, subject to 
regulations and restrictions under laws that the General Assembly enacts. Contingent on 
passage and ratification of the constitutional amendment, the bill makes related 
modifications and additions to existing General Assembly intent in statute that residents 
have a right to hunt and a right to fish, subject to regulations and restrictions under laws 
that the General Assembly enacts. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  The bill does not directly affect State finances. 
  
Local Effect:  The bill does not directly affect local government finances. 
 
Small Business Effect:  None. 
 
 
Analysis 
 
Bill Summary:  The proposed constitutional amendment establishes under the Maryland 
Declaration of Rights that every person must have the right to hunt, fish, and harvest 
wildlife, including the right to use traditional methods to hunt, fish, and harvest wildlife, 
subject to regulations and restrictions under laws that the General Assembly enacts, to  
(1) promote wildlife conservation and management and (2) preserve the future of hunting 
and fishing. These rights may not be construed to limit the application of any provision of 
law relating to trespass or property rights. 
 
Contingent on passage of the constitutional amendment and its ratification by the voters of 
the State, the bill modifies and adds to existing General Assembly intent expressed in

statute – under Title 4 (Fish and Fisheries) and Title 10 (Wildlife) of the Natural Resources 
Article – that residents of the State have a right to hunt and a right to fish, subject to 
regulations and restrictions under laws that the General Assembly enacts. The bill modifies 
that intent to include that residents of the State have the right to use traditional methods to 
hunt and fish. The bill also adds General Assembly intent that (1) the right to hunt and the 
right to fish must be forever preserved for the public good and (2) hunting and fishing must 
be preferred means of managing and controlling wildlife in the State.  
 
Current Law:  Chapters 295 and 296 of 2021 established, under Titles 4 and 10 of the 
Natural Resources Article, findings and intent of the General Assembly regarding hunting 
and fishing. Specifically, under Chapters 295 and 296, the General Assembly finds that: 
 
 
hunting and fishing are valued parts of the State’s cultural and social heritage that 
provide unique recreational benefits to residents of the State; and 
 
hunting and fishing (1) play an important part in the State’s economy by 
contributing to tax revenues and employment and (2) support the conservation, 
preservation, and management of the State’s fisheries and wildlife, natural areas, 
and water and related resources (in the case of hunting, through the receipt of federal 
funds). 
 
Chapters 295 and 296 establish the intent of the General Assembly that residents of the 
State have a right to hunt and a right to fish, subject to regulations and restrictions under 
laws that the General Assembly enacts.
