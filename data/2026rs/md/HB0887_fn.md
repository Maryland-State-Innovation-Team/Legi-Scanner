Ways and Means and Appropriations 
 
 
Video Lottery Terminals - Distribution of Local Impact Grants - Allocation for 
Fair Hill Purses 
 
 
This bill requires, from fiscal 2027 through 2029, $200,000 of the local impact grants 
distributed to Cecil County from video lottery terminal (VLT) proceeds to annually be used 
for purses offered for races held at the Fair Hill Natural Resources Management Area. The 
bill takes effect July 1, 2026. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  None. 
  
Local Effect:  For FY 2027 through 2029, $200,000 of local impact grants to Cecil County 
for improvements primarily in communities surrounding the video lottery facility in 
Cecil County is redirected to purses offered for races held at the Fair Hill Natural Resources 
Management Area. 
 
Small Business Effect:  Minimal. 
 
 
Analysis 
 
Current Law:  Generally, 6% of VLT proceeds at each facility is distributed to the Purse 
Dedication Account. The Fair Hill Natural Resources Management Area receives 
$100,000 annually from the amount provided to thoroughbred purses from the Purse 
Dedication Account.  
 
From the VLT proceeds at each facility, generally 5.5% is distributed as local impact grants 
to local governments in which a video lottery facility is operating. The grants are

The local impact grant funding is subject to additional requirements governing the 
distribution of the funding among local governments and the use of the funding, as well as 
a requirement that a local development council be established in each geographic area 
where a facility is located to advise the local governments in their planning of how to spend 
the funding. Generally, local impact grants must be used for infrastructure improvements; 
facilities; public safety; sanitation; economic and community development, including 
housing; and other public services and improvements primarily in the communities in 
immediate proximity to the video lottery facilities. 
 
Local Fiscal Effect:  Cecil County local impact grant expenditures for public services and 
improvements primarily in the communities in immediate proximity to the Cecil County 
video lottery facility decrease by $200,000 annually from fiscal 2027 through 2029.
