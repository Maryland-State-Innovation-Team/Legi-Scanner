1 WHEREAS, In Szeliga v. Lamone and Parrott v. Lamone, the Circuit Court for Anne
2 Arundel County held that Article III, § 4 districting standards apply to congressional
3 districts, relying on Articles 7, 24, and 40 of the Declaration of Rights, though no Maryland
4 constitutional provision expressly addresses congressional districting; and

5 WHEREAS, This amendment clarifies the long–standing understanding and case
6 law that Article III, § 4 of the Maryland Constitution governs only General Assembly
7 districting plans, not congressional districting plans; and

8 WHEREAS, Preserving Maryland’s cohesive communities of interest connected by
9 shared civic, social, economic, political subdivisions, and other common interests without
10 unnecessary fragmentation is a legitimate, neutral consideration in congressional
11 districting; and

12 WHEREAS, Nothing in this amendment establishes a demographic target, requires
13 proportional representation, guarantees any electoral outcome, or authorizes
14 discrimination prohibited by federal law; and

15 WHEREAS, This amendment is not intended to provide new grounds to challenge
16 the plan enacted by Chapter 16 of the Acts of 2022; now, therefore,

17 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND,
18 (Three–fifths of all the members elected to each of the two Houses concurring), That it be
19 proposed that the Maryland Constitution read as follows:

20 Article III – Legislative Department

21 4.

22 Each legislative district FOR THE ELECTION OF A MEMBER OF THE SENATE AND
23 MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE OF DELEGATES shall consist of adjoining territory, be compact
24 in form, and of substantially equal population. Due regard shall be given to natural
25 boundaries and the boundaries of political subdivisions.

26 62.

27 (A) THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY SHALL HAVE THE POWER TO GRANT
28 ORIGINAL JURISDICTION TO THE SUPREME COURT OF MARYLAND TO REVIEW THE
29 CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICTING PLAN OF THE STATE.

30 (B) NOTHING IN THIS ARTICLE OR ELSEWHERE IN THIS CONSTITUTION,
31 INCLUDING THE DECLARATION OF RIGHTS, PROVIDES APPLICABLE CRITERIA FOR
32 THE BOUNDARIES OF A CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICTING PLAN.
