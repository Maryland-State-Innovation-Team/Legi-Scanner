1 Article – Health – General

2 7–101.

3 (a) In this title the following words have the meanings indicated.

4 (g) “Developmental disability” means a severe chronic disability of an
5 individual that:

6 (1) Is attributable to a physical or mental impairment, other than the
7 sole diagnosis of mental illness, or to a combination of mental and physical impairments;

8 (2) Is manifested before the individual attains the age of 22;

9 (3) Is likely to continue indefinitely;

10 (4) Results in an inability to live independently without external
11 support or continuing and regular assistance; and

12 (5) Reflects the need for a combination and sequence of special,
13 interdisciplinary, or generic care, treatment, or other services that are individually
14 planned and coordinated for the individual.

15 7–404.2.

16 (A) AN INDIVIDUAL SHALL BE ELIGIBLE TO RECEIVE SERVICES FROM THE
17 DEPARTMENT UNDER THIS TITLE IF:

18 (A) (1) IN THIS SECTION THE FOLLOWING WORDS HAVE THE MEANINGS
19 INDICATED.

20 (2) “DEPENDENT” MEANS AN INDIVIDUAL WITH DISABILITIES WHO:

21 (I) RELIES ON SUPPORT FROM AN IMMEDIATE FAMILY
22 MEMBER WHO IS A RESIDENT OF THE STATE; OR

23 (II) HAS AN IMMEDIATE FAMILY MEMBER WHO IS A RESIDENT
24 OF THE STATE AND ACTS AS THE INDIVIDUAL’S LEGAL GUARDIAN.

25 (3) “IMMEDIATE FAMILY MEMBER” MEANS A SPOUSE, A PARENT, AN
26 ADOPTIVE PARENT, A STEPPARENT, A GRANDPARENT, A STEPGRANDPARENT, A
27 SIBLING, A STEPSIBLING, A CHILD, OR A STEPCHILD.

28 (B) THE DEPARTMENT SHALL PROVIDE AN EXPEDITED MEDICAID ELIGIBILITY
29 DETERMINATION FOR DEVELOPMENTAL DISABILITIES SERVICES
30 FOR PURPOSES OF ENROLLMENT IN THE COMMUNITY
31 PATHWAYS WAIVER TO AN INDIVIDUAL IF:

32 (1) THE INDIVIDUAL:

33 (I) IS A DEPENDENT OF AN IMMEDIATE FAMILY MEMBER;

34 (II) RECEIVED HOME– AND COMMUNITY–BASED WAIVER
35 SERVICES OR SIMILAR SERVICES UNDER A SIMILAR ASSISTANCE PROGRAM IN
36 ANOTHER STATE FOR THE IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING 10 YEARS; AND

37 (III) MOVED TO THE STATE TO LIVE WITH THE IMMEDIATE
38 FAMILY MEMBER ON THE DEATH OR INCAPACITATION OF THE INDIVIDUAL’S
39 CARETAKER IN THE OTHER STATE; AND

40 (2) THE IMMEDIATE FAMILY MEMBER ON WHOM THE INDIVIDUAL IS
41 DEPENDENT HAS RESIDED IN THE STATE FOR THE IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING 2
42 YEARS.
