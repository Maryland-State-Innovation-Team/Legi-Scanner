County Boards of Education - Artificial Intelligence Training Program - 
Requirement 
 
 
This bill requires the Maryland State Department of Education (MSDE) to develop or 
approve an artificial intelligence (AI) training program for members of local boards of 
education that includes a foundational training as well as continuing annual training. The 
bill specifies required elements of the training and establishes a timeline in which members 
of local boards of education must complete the training. MSDE must publish on its website 
a report of school board members who did not complete the training as required under the 
bill for the previous year. On or before October 1, 2028, and each October 1 every 
two years thereafter, MSDE must submit a report to the Governor and the 
General Assembly on the effectiveness of the AI training program. The bill takes effect 
July 1, 2026. 
 
 
Fiscal Summary 
 
State Effect:  General fund expenditures increase by $169,100 in FY 2027 which reflect 
one-time contractual services to develop the training as well as staffing to oversee the 
training. Future years reflect the salary of a part-time employee to manage the program. 
No effect on revenues.

Analysis 
 
Bill Summary:  The foundational training must contain:   
 
 
fundamentals of AI, including the capabilities, limitations, and risk of AI; 
 
AI technology use policy development, including safety and ethical considerations 
by age and grade level; 
 
student privacy and data protection laws; and  
 
AI technology procurement guidelines and oversight. 
 
The continuing annual training must contain a summary of the topics covered in the 
foundational training as well as developments in AI technology, evolving practices for use 
in educational settings, and new guidance. 
 
The training program developed or approved by MSDE may be offered by an organization 
responsible for the professional development and education of members of local boards of 
education, and may be in person, online, or in a hybrid format.  
 
Within one year after the first day of a local board member’s term of office, the board 
member must complete the foundational AI training. A board member who is in office on 
the date of enactment of the bill must complete the foundational training on or before 
June 30, 2027, and the annual training on or before June 30 in subsequent years. On 
completion of the training, a board member must submit a copy of their certificate of 
completion to the president of the local board of education on which they serve as well as 
MSDE; however, a local board of education or MSDE may establish processes to 
automatically record a board member’s completion of the training instead. 
 
Current Law:   
 
Artificial Intelligence 
 
“Artificial intelligence” means a machine-based system that (1) can, for a given set of 
human-defined objectives, make predictions, recommendations, or decisions influencing 
real or virtual environments; (2) uses machine and human-based inputs to perceive real and 
virtual environments and abstracts those perceptions into models through analysis in an 
automated manner; and (3) uses model inference to formulate options for information or 
action.

Required Trainings for Local Boards of Education 
 
Current law requires members of local boards of education to complete an antibias training 
at least once during their term of office. Additionally, chair and vice chair of local boards 
of education must complete a school leadership training program. 
 
State Expenditures:  General fund expenditures increase by $169,053 in fiscal 2027 which 
reflect one-time contractual services to develop the training ($115,000) as well as staffing 
to oversee the training ($54,053). Future year expenditures, which total $60,996 in 
fiscal 2028 and $70,002 in fiscal 2031, reflect the salary of a part-time employee to manage 
the program. 
 
Development of Training 
 
MSDE expects to hire a vendor to develop the AI training on MSDE’s Canvas Learning 
Management System, which will allow for members of local boards of education to 
complete the training online. Accordingly, general fund expenditures increase by 
approximately $115,000 starting in fiscal 2027 for one-time costs to pay a contractor to 
develop the training and ensure it meets all the requirements of the bill.  
 
AI Coordinator 
 
While MSDE intends to contract out the development of the training, the department will 
still require a part-time position to oversee compliance and meet the bill’s requirement for 
updates to trainings for future years. Accordingly, general fund expenditures increase by 
$54,053 in fiscal 2027, which accounts for a 90-day start-up delay from the bill’s  
July 1, 2026 effective date. This estimate reflects the cost of hiring one part-time 
AI Coordinator to update and edit future training materials, report on board member 
completion of the training, manage logistics, and produce the biennial report on the training 
as required by the bill. It includes a salary, fringe benefits, one-time start-up costs, and 
ongoing expenses.
