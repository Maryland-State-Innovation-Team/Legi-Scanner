1 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND,
2 That:

3 (a) There is a Workgroup on Transportation Contract Approval Authority.

4 (b) The Workgroup consists of the following members:

5 (1) one member of the House of Delegates, appointed by the Speaker of
6 the House;

7 (2) one member of the Senate of Maryland, appointed by the President
8 of the Senate;

9 (3) the Comptroller, or the Comptroller’s designee;

10 (4) the State Treasurer, or the State Treasurer’s designee;

11 (5) the Secretary of Transportation, or the Secretary’s designee;

12 (6) the Secretary of Social and Economic Mobility, or the Secretary’s
13 designee;

14 (7) a representative appointed by the Maryland Association of Counties;
15 and

16 (8) the following members appointed by the Governor:

17 (i) a representative of the engineering and design services
18 industry;

19 (ii) a representative of the transportation construction and
20 materials industry;

21 (iii) a member of the business community who has been certified
22 as a Disadvantaged Business Enterprise and as a Minority Business Enterprise; and

23 (iv) a representative of a State labor organization.

24 (c) The Governor shall designate the chair of the Workgroup.

25 (d) The Department of Transportation and the Board of Public Works shall
26 jointly provide staff for the Workgroup.

1 (e) A member of the Workgroup:

2 (1) may not receive compensation as a member of the Workgroup; but

3 (2) is entitled to reimbursement for expenses under the Standard State
4 Travel Regulations, as provided in the State budget.

5 (f) The Workgroup shall:

6 (1) study the existing approval authority structure for capital
7 expenditures by the Department of Transportation and the Maryland Transportation
8 Authority in connection with State roads, bridges, or highways, including a review of
9 the justifications for the existing approval authority structure and whether the
10 structure continues to provide public benefit;

11 (2) study the effect of altering the approval authority structure for
12 capital expenditures by the Department of Transportation and the Maryland
13 Transportation Authority in connection with State roads, bridges, or highways,
14 including whether to require approval of the award of new contracts and change orders
15 for these projects by the Board of Public Works process;

16 (3) study the purpose, impact, and public value of the Board of Public
17 Works’ approval process for capital expenditures by the Department of Transportation
18 and the Maryland Transportation Authority in connection with State roads, bridges, or
19 highways;

20 (4) analyze alternative options for review and approval by the Board of
21 Public Works for the award of new contracts and change orders for road, bridge, or
22 highway projects that include:

23 (i) advance notice and opportunity to review and comment on
24 upcoming contracts conducted by the Department of Transportation or the Maryland
25 Transportation Authority, and the appropriate stage of the contract for these activities;

26 (ii) public reporting of contract and change order awards made by
27 the Department of Transportation or the Maryland Transportation Authority; or

28 (iii) appropriate thresholds for new contracts and contract change
29 orders or modifications related to capital expenditures by the Department of
30 Transportation and the Maryland Transportation Authority in connection with State
31 roads, bridges, or highways;
