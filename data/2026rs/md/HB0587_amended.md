2 Procurement – Department of Transportation and Maryland Transportation

1 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND,
2 That:

3 (a) There is a Workgroup on Transportation Contract Approval Authority.

4 (b) The Workgroup consists of the following members:

5 (1) one member of the House of Delegates, appointed by the Speaker of
6 the House;

7 (2) one member of the Senate of Maryland, appointed by the President
8 of the Senate;

9 (3) the Comptroller, or the Comptroller’s designee;

10 (4) the State Treasurer, or the State Treasurer’s designee;

11 (5) the Secretary of Transportation, or the Secretary’s designee;

12 (6) the Secretary of Social and Economic Mobility, or the Secretary’s
13 designee;

14 (7) a representative appointed by the Maryland Association of Counties;
15 and

16 (8) the following members appointed by the Governor:

17 (i) a representative of the engineering and design services
18 industry;

19 (ii) a representative of the transportation construction and
20 materials industry;

21 (iii) a member of the business community who has been certified
22 as a Disadvantaged Business Enterprise and as a Minority Business Enterprise; and

23 (iv) a representative of a State labor organization.

24 (c) The Governor shall designate the chair of the Workgroup.

25 (d) The Department of Transportation and the Board of Public Works shall
26 jointly provide staff for the Workgroup.

27 (e) A member of the Workgroup:

28 (1) may not receive compensation as a member of the Workgroup; but

29 (2) is entitled to reimbursement for expenses under the Standard State
30 Travel Regulations, as provided in the State budget.

1 (f) The Workgroup shall:

2 (1) study the existing approval authority structure for capital
3 expenditures by the Department of Transportation and the Maryland Transportation
4 Authority in connection with State roads, bridges, or highways, including a review of
5 the justifications for the existing approval authority structure and whether the
6 structure continues to provide public benefit;

7 (2) study the effect of altering the approval authority structure for
8 capital expenditures by the Department of Transportation and the Maryland
9 Transportation Authority in connection with State roads, bridges, or highways,
10 including whether to require approval of the award of new contracts and change orders
11 for these projects by the Board of Public Works process;

12 (3) study the purpose, impact, and public value of the Board of Public
13 Works’ approval process for capital expenditures by the Department of Transportation
14 and the Maryland Transportation Authority in connection with State roads, bridges, or
15 highways;

16 (4) analyze alternative options for review and approval by the Board of
17 Public Works for the award of new contracts and change orders for road, bridge, or
18 highway projects that include:

19 (i) advance notice and opportunity to review and comment on
20 upcoming contracts conducted by the Department of Transportation or the Maryland
21 Transportation Authority, and the appropriate stage of the contract for these activities;

22 (ii) public reporting of contract and change order awards made by
23 the Department of Transportation or the Maryland Transportation Authority; or

24 (iii) appropriate thresholds for new contracts and contract change
25 orders or modifications related to capital expenditures by the Department of
26 Transportation and the Maryland Transportation Authority in connection with State
27 roads, bridges, or highways;

28 (5) examine the impact on contract award and completion timeline;

29 (6) evaluate the effect of expanding the current delegated authority for
30 capital expenditures by the Department of Transportation and the Maryland
31 Transportation Authority in connection with State roads, bridges, or highways to
32 contracts and change orders for maintenance and capital construction to support service
33 on Maryland Transit Administration fixed guideway systems and associated transit
34 facilities and vehicles; and

35 (7) evaluate the impact of the existing approval authority structure and
36 any alternative approval authority structures on the ability to secure federal funding
37 for roads, bridges, or highways.
