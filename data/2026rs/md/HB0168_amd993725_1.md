AMENDMENTS TO HOUSE BILL 168
(Third Reading File Bill)

AMENDMENT NO. 1
On page 1, in line 4, after “of” insert “authorizing a county board of education to
consider the provision of educator workforce housing as a school purpose under certain
provisions of law;”.

AMENDMENT NO. 2
On page 2, in line 12, strike the brackets; strike beginning with “THE” in line 12
down through “COUNTY” in line 14; and strike in their entirety lines 16 through 18,
inclusive, and substitute:

“(2) FOR PURPOSES OF THIS SUBTITLE, A COUNTY BOARD MAY
CONSIDER THE PROVISION OF EDUCATOR WORKFORCE HOUSING, AS DEFINED IN
§ 1–101 OF THE HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT ARTICLE, AS A
SCHOOL PURPOSE.”.

On page 4, in line 20, after “SYSTEM” insert “WHO ARE NOT ADMINISTRATORS
RESPONSIBLE FOR THE STRATEGIC DIRECTION AND DAILY OPERATIONS OF THE
LOCAL SCHOOL SYSTEM OR A PUBLIC SCHOOL”.
