2 HOUSE BILL 663

1 (A) ON OR BEFORE JULY 1, 2026, AND EVERY 6 MONTHS THEREAFTER, THE
2 DEPARTMENT SHALL REPORT TO THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY, IN ACCORDANCE WITH
3 § 2–1257 OF THE STATE GOVERNMENT ARTICLE, ON:

4 (1) ANY CHANGES IN FEDERAL ENVIRONMENTAL POLICY ON
5 GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS THAT HAVE BEEN DEEMED LEGALLY VALID BY A FINAL
6 DECISION OF A FEDERAL COURT, INCLUDING ANY CHANGE THAT:

7 (I) RESCINDS A FINDING THAT THE GREENHOUSE GAS
8 EMISSIONS FROM NEW MOTOR VEHICLES AND ENGINES CAUSE AIR POLLUTION THAT
9 MAY ENDANGER PUBLIC HEALTH OR WELFARE;

10 (II) REPEALS GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS STANDARDS FOR
11 MOTOR VEHICLES AND ENGINES; OR

12 (III) REPEALS GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS STANDARDS FOR
13 STATIONARY SOURCES; AND

14 (IV) IMPACTS THE DEPARTMENT’S ADMINISTRATION OF ANY
15 FEDERALLY DELEGATED AUTHORITY; OR

16 (V) RELATES TO NUCLEAR POWER; AND

17 (2) IF THERE HAS BEEN A CHANGE IN FEDERAL ENVIRONMENTAL
18 POLICY ON GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS THAT IS REQUIRED TO BE REPORTED
19 UNDER PARAGRAPH (1) OF THIS SECTION, THE STEPS THE STATE IS TAKING IN
20 RESPONSE TO THE CHANGE.

21 (B) (1) THE INITIAL REPORT SUBMITTED UNDER SUBSECTION (A) OF
22 THIS SECTION SHALL DESCRIBE ANY CHANGES IN FEDERAL ENVIRONMENTAL
23 POLICY ON GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS THAT HAVE OCCURRED SINCE JANUARY 1,
24 2024.

25 (2) EACH REPORT SUBMITTED AFTER THE INITIAL REPORT SHALL
26 DESCRIBE ANY CHANGES IN FEDERAL ENVIRONMENTAL POLICY ON GREENHOUSE
27 GAS EMISSIONS THAT HAVE OCCURRED DURING THE IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING 6
28 MONTH PERIOD.

29 SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That this Act shall take effect June
30 1, 2026. It shall remain effective for a period of 3 years and, at the end of May 31, 2029,
31 this Act, with no further action required by the General Assembly, shall be abrogated and
32 of no further force and effect.
